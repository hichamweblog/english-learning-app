SpaceX and Its Plan to 
Colonize Mars 
Have you ever said to yourself, “I’d just love to 
get away from it all”? Well, how about going to 
Mars? Seriously. Elon Musk, the CEO of SpaceX, 
is aiming to colonize Mars by 2026.  
SpaceX has actually made significant strides 
toward putting humans on the red planet. Elon 
Musk’s goal is for humankind to become a 
multi-planet species so that we can survive 
even a catastrophic event on earth. If you 
spend a few minutes perusing the recent 
achievements of SpaceX, you have to believe 
that it might just be possible. 
The problem with rocket ships is that they cost 
a pretty penny to build and can’t easily be 
reused. However, SpaceX was able to send a 
rocket to replenish supplies at the International 
Space Station, then return it to earth – becoming 
the first private company to do so. SpaceX now 
has a $1.6 billion contract with NASA to deliver 
cargo to the space station, as well as many other 
customers vying for its services.

Elon Musk, who is originally from South Africa, sold 
his start-up software company Zip2 for $300 million 
when he was only 28 years old. This was just 
peanuts compared to when his next company, 
PayPal, was sold to eBay for $1.5 billion. After 
dabbling in a hedonistic lifestyle, Musk quickly 
realized that he needed a more meaningful project 
to sink his teeth into. He began pouring his energy 
and fortune into a couple of incredibly ambitious 
start-ups.  
First he founded Tesla, which aims to replace all the 
cars in the world with electric vehicles (the change 
is already underway). Then came SpaceX, which he 
formed in 2002 and funded with $100M of his own
