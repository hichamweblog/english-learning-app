Seven Unusual Hotels 
You could always go to a regular old hotel, stay in 
a nice clean room, and eat generic food in their 
dining room… but what if you want something a 
bit more memorable? 
There are hundreds of unique hotels in the world, 
but in the interests of keeping things concise, 
here are seven that caught my eye. 
1. Hotel Kakslauttanen in Finland 
If you’ve ever seen the northern lights, you 
know that they’re breathtaking. Now, 
imagine seeing them from inside a 
transparent igloo. 
The Hotel Kakslauttanen is located in 
northern Finland, and believe it or not, all 
the cabins are made out of ice, or glass that 
looks like ice. They offer a great view, but 
needless to say not a whole lot of privacy.

3. Das Park Hotel in Austria 
The rooms in this hotel are built inside 
concrete sewer pipes on the banks of the 
Danube River. Each pipe segment is 2.5 
meters long, 2 meters in diameter, and 
boasts comfy futons with blankets. And it 
won’t cost you an arm and a leg – the 
guests actually determine the rates. 
4. Dog Bark Park in Idaho 
It’s referred to as the world’s largest 
beagle. This hotel is built in the shape of a 
huge dog, nine meters long. A room 
contains a queen size bed along with two 
twin mattresses in the loft. You’ll get dog-
shaped cookies, too! Everything is dog-
themed. Oddly enough, though, you can’t 
bring pets without prior approval. It’s 
relatively inexpensive - $98 per night for 
double occupancy.

This tiny little two-room hotel is made from 
one of the last planes out of Vietnam. It sleeps 
four in the body of the plane, and four in the

6. Kadir’s Tree Houses in Turkey 
On Turkey’s island of Olimpos, you can stay in a 
little wooden bungalow that is nestled in the 
trees. Kadir’s offers over a hundred such 
bungalows, and is known as a great party spot. 
Would you believe that you can stay there for 
just $20 per night? 
7. Kokopelli’s Cave Bed & Breakfast in New 
Mexico 
You might think that sleeping in a cave isn’t 
luxurious – but you’d be wrong. At Kokopelli’s, 
you can stay in a fully-furnished room that’s 
dug into an ancient sandstone cliff. Your room 
will be carpeted and have all the creature 
comforts you’d expect, and you can stay there 
for just $280 per night. 
So if you’re looking for an interesting experience for 
your next vacation, consider staying at these one-of-
a-kind hotels – you’ll certainly never forget your stay!
