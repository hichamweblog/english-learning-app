Disastrous Dating 
Pet Peeves 
Dating can be fun and exciting, but the 
foibles of the opposite sex can also drive 
you crazy. Let’s look at the top dating gripes 
from both a male and a female perspective.  
Men on women 
1: Tweeting or Facebooking over dinner is a 
big no-no. If you really want to exasperate 
your date, take a picture of your expensive 
entrée, nibble at it, and then claim you can’t 
possibly eat any more because it’s full of 
calories! 
2: If you are visibly upset about something, 
and we ask what’s wrong, don’t just say 
“nothing” – men prefer candor to guessing 
games. The worst thing you can do is to 
conceal or bottle up your emotions, only to 
unleash them at a later time when the guy 
probably won’t even remember the incident 
you’re still riled up over. 
3: Talking about your ex – regardless of 
whether you’re singing his praises or 
trashing him. This is especially bad on a 
first date – when a girl goes on and on 
about her ex-boyfriend, it’s a major red flag.

4: Overdoing the makeup can also be a 
turnoff. Most men actually prefer a more 
natural look in which makeup is used 
tastefully to accent your best features. 
Don’t plaster on more makeup than 
normal. 
Women on men 
1: Not listening. We all know that men 
sometimes space out, go on autopilot and 
interject with the occasional “yeah” or 
“hmm.” This doesn’t fool women one bit, 
and it’s very bad form to daydream about 
the weekend sports games while a woman 
is trying to get to know you. If this 
becomes a habit, it can be a deal-breaker 
in a relationship. 
2: Coming off as a ‘know-it-all’. When men 
are nervous or insecure, they sometimes 
try too hard to impress. This can manifest 
itself in bragging or even belittling their 
date’s experiences and opinions in order to 
make themselves seem superior. Instead of 
oversharing about your achievements, 
offer little snippets of information to 
create a little intrigue. If your date is 
yawning and checking her phone, it’s a 
sign that you’re talking too much.

3: Ordering for your date. This is not 
chivalrous, it’s just arrogant – especially 
on a first date when you have little to no 
idea what your female companion likes or 
dislikes. Your date can fend for herself, 
and that includes ordering her own dish. 
4: Asking about sex too soon. Although 
nowadays we are all far less inhibited 
when it comes to talking about what goes 
on between the sheets, don’t jump the 
gun. Early on in a relationship – and 
especially on the first encounter – it’s 
rather sleazy to ask whether a woman 
enjoys a particular sex act or not! 
Communication while dating can also be a 
can of worms. Women often find it 
infuriating how long men can take to 
respond to text messages and how vague 
they can be about plans. Meanwhile, men 
can be hesitant to text or call too much for 
fear of coming on too strong, especially 
early in a relationship when they don’t yet 
know where they stand. 
It simply takes patience and empathy for 
men and women to put up with each 
other’s flaws and have a healthy, mutually 
satisfying relationship.
