The World’s Most Expensive Foods 
If you could spend an unlimited amount of money 
on something delicious to eat, what would you 
choose? If you have money to burn, you might 
like to pick out one of the world’s most expensive 
foods. Which one of these pleases your palate? 
Bagels 
For a thousand dollars, the chef of the Westin 
Hotel in New York will present you with a 
delicately toasted bagel, topped with goji berry 
and white truffle cream cheese, infused with a 
jelly made from Riesling wine. What makes it so 
expensive? It’s the truffles – white truffles are the 
most expensive in the world. They grow only in 
Alba, Italy, under certain oak trees. 
Potatoes 
Would you believe that potatoes could cost $45 
per pound? It’s true. The La Bonnotte potato only 
grows on a particular French island. It has to be 
harvested by hand, and the growing season is a 
mere ten days in the month of May. You can buy 
it only in the ritziest restaurants in the 
Netherlands and France.

Mushrooms 
You won’t find matsutake mushrooms in your 
local supermarket. They’re found only in Japan, 
only in autumn, and they can’t be cultivated. The 
trees under which they grow are becoming 
scarce, and these mushrooms cost in the 
neighborhood of a thousand dollars for a pound. 
Frittata 
Another thousand-dollar dish is the breakfast 
frittata that upscale restaurants create using the 
world’s third-most expensive variety of caviar – 
ten ounces of it, at a cost to the restaurant of $65 
per ounce – combined with lobster and, of course, 
eggs. 
Samundari Khazana Curry 
For $3,200, you can dine on the most expensive 
curry in the world, available from Bombay 
Brassiere. It consists of white truffle, Devon crab, 
beluga caviar and half a tomato, garnished in 
gold leaf. Add to that a Scottish lobster (also 
coated in gold), quail eggs stuffed with caviar, 
and abalone, and you have a dish fit for a king… 
and it’s likely that only a king can afford it!

Wagyu Steak 
Japanese Wagyu cattle are fed beer and 
massaged by hand to ensure the superior 
marbling and tenderness of the meat. The fat 
content is through the roof, so people at risk for 
heart problems should likely avoid it.  
If you’re in good health, though, and want to taste 
a steak from the world’s most pampered cow, 
you can get a Wagyu steak at Craftsteak in New 
York for just $2,800.  
Pizza Royale 007  
You’d think that $4,200 would get you more than 
just a twelve-inch pie, but not when it’s topped 
with cognac-marinated lobster, champagne-
soaked caviar, smoked salmon from Scotland, 
venison, and the finest prosciutto.  
Add to that a garnish of 24-carat gold flakes, and 
you have the world’s most expensive pizza. 
Yubari Melons 
If you think something as simple as a piece of 
fruit couldn’t possibly be expensive – think again! 
The most expensive melons ever sold were a pair 
of Yubari cantaloupes that were grown in 2008.

The Golden Opulence Sundae 
Time for dessert – it’ll run you about $1,000. The 
Golden Opulence Sundae is made from Tahitian 
vanilla ice cream topped with chocolate syrup 
made from melting one of the world’s most 
expensive chocolates. The sundae is also 
adorned with caviar, exotic candied fruit, 
truffles, marzipan cherries, and edible 23-carat 
gold leaf.  
After meals like these, your stomach will be full, 
but your wallet will be lighter!
