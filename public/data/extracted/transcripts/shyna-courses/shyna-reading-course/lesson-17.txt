Interesting Recent Inventions 
 
Think our modern world already has everything 
you could possibly imagine? Think again. 
Human creativity knows no bounds, and new 
products and technologies are being invented all 
the time. Here are a few of the notable things 
invented recently. 
 
The Hoverboard 
 
Who hasn’t dreamed of being Marty McFly from 
Back To The Future II, zipping around Biff on his 
flying skateboard? Given that the 1989 film 
imagined the “future” in the year 2015 (our 
present and soon to be past), this product was 
developed in the nick of time.  
 
However, the technology is not quite up to par 
compared to the thrilling device in the science 
fiction movie. There are limitations – for 
instance, the board will only levitate over 
conductive materials like copper and 
aluminum, flies just an inch off the ground, and 
can only operate for fifteen minutes at a time.  
 
Also, only ten prototypes are on the market, 
all at the whopping sum of $10,000—meaning 
only someone like Michael J. Fox himself can 
afford it.

Wireless Electricity 
 
We are already doing away with so many 
cords: telephone cords, Internet cords—
why not eliminate the need for cords 
altogether?  
 
WiTricity, a Massachusetts company, wants 
to do just that – without the use of batteries. 
They have developed a plug-in coil that 
produces a magnetic field, which can power 
all electronic devices up to several meters 
away – even through walls. So far, this 
technology has been applied to Toyota 
electric cars and Intel PCs, but WiTricity is 
looking at the big picture. So say goodbye 
to the days of tripping over your power 
cords. We’ll soon be operating completely 
wirelessly.  
 
3-D Printing 
 
3-D printing has exploded over the past 
few years, and it is bound to revolutionize 
nearly all types of production. These 
printers are able to apply successive layers 
of glass, metal, plastic, and other materials 
in order to build objects designed on a 
digital blueprint.

Alert Ring 
 
You know the feeling: your cell phone is buried 
somewhere in your purse, and because you’re 
expecting a call or an email, you periodically feel 
buzzes from within your bag.  
 
After rummaging through all your belongings, 
you finally locate your phone and discover the 
buzz was a figment of your imagination, 
generated by the expectation of a message.  
 
This new invention, a ring whose stone blinks 
when the wearer receives an email, call, or text, 
will end the uncertainty and help you stay on top 
of your messages.

The selfie stick is not exactly a revolutionary idea – 
a photographer in 1925 even used a stick to 
extend the range of his camera. However, the 
modern version of the product hit the market at 
the height of the selfie craze – and has been 
selling like hotcakes.
