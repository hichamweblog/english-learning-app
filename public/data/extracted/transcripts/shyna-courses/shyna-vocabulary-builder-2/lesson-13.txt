Lesson 13: Appearance & Personal 
Care 
Hair 
A small haircut is called a trim. Anyone with long hair should get a trim 
regularly in order to get rid of split ends (when the end of each strand of hair 
divides in two). 
To change the color of your hair, you can dye it a different color. Sometimes 
people dye only a few small pieces of hair – this is called getting highlights.

An afro is a large, curly style often used by people of African descent. Bangs 
are short pieces of hair that cover your forehead. An updo is any style in 
which the hair is pinned up on top of the head; updos are often used for 
formal events.  
Hair with layers has pieces of different lengths. A braid is created by weaving 
three sections of hair together, and a ponytail is simply holding the hair 
together with an elastic band. You can use hair gel to make your hair spiked, 
and dreadlocks are a hairstyle in which the hair is twisted into matted 
sections. 
If someone has lost their hair, they can use a toupee or a wig. The difference 
is that a toupee covers a bald spot and is usually used by men; a wig is a 
complete hair replacement and is usually used by women.

The hair that grows on your body is called “body hair,” and there are several 
different ways to remove it: 
• shave with a razor – men usually do this to their faces 
 
• pluck hairs with tweezers – women often do this to their eyebrows 
 
• wax – remove the hair by applying hot wax, letting it cool, then ripping 
it off

• laser hair removal – permanent or semi-permanent hair removal 
 
Skin 
Teenagers often have a skin problem called acne, in which red bumps called 
pimples appear on the face. When you get older, the skin problems change – 
you get wrinkles (lines) and age spots (small dark areas) instead.

These small brown marks on the face are called freckles, and a single dark 
spot which can be anywhere on the body is called a mole.

Makeup can help hide imperfections of the skin, and there’s also a spa 
treatment called a facial in which a special cream mask is put on the face to 
heal and help the skin. For serious problems, you can see a doctor specializing 
in the skin, called a dermatologist.

Body 
Your idea and opinion of how your body looks and 
how attractive you are is called your body image. If 
you’re not satisfied with your body, this can be 
damaging to your self-esteem – your general feeling 
about yourself. 
Women often worry about their figure – that’s a 
general word for the shape of your body – and men 
tend to be concerned with their physique – the 
proportions of your body, especially the muscles. 
Men who want to get more muscular can lift 
weights or take supplements in an attempt to “bulk up” – become more 
muscular.

To lose weight, the best strategy is proper nutrition and exercise – but 
unfortunately some people develop eating disorders like anorexia (eating 
extremely little food and not enough nutrients) and bulimia (vomiting your 
food immediately after eating it). 
Another way to change the appearance of your body is to get plastic surgery 
or cosmetic surgery. Some of the most common types are: 
• breast implants – to make the breasts bigger 
• liposuction – surgically removing fat from inside the body 
• a tummy tuck – smoothing and stretching the skin of the stomach 
• a facelift – removes wrinkles and makes the face look younger 
• a nose job – changing the shape of the nose
