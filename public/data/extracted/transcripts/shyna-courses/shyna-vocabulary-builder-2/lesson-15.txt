Lesson 15: Relationships 
Getting Together 
Let’s begin with friendship. If a man and a woman have a platonic 
relationship, it means they are only friends; there is no romantic relationship.  
Maybe they just didn’t have a spark or 
chemistry – both these words are used to 
describe romantic connection that feels 
exciting and natural. 
Sometimes a person develops a crush on 
one of their friends – if you have a crush on 
someone, it means you have secret romantic 
feelings for that person. 
Some couples get together through a blind date – that’s when they didn’t 
know each other before the date. A mutual friend often “sets up” the blind 
date. 
Casual Relationships 
A brief romantic or sexual relationship that’s not serious is called a fling. A 
fling can last anywhere from a couple weeks to a few months. Even shorter 
than a fling is a one-night stand – having just one night of sex with someone. 
It’s common to use the expression “hook up” for an intimate encounter. 
Sometimes hooking up leads to the beginning of a relationship, and other 
times it’s just for fun. There’s also a type of relationship called friends with 
benefits – two friends who have sex with each other because they enjoy it, but 
they don’t have a commitment or romantic relationship. 
Someone who has casual sex with a lot of different people can be described as 
promiscuous. Another expression for having sex with many partners is 
sleeping around.

Serious Relationships 
There are a number of ways to say that two people are in a committed 
relationship. You can say that he/she is taken or that the two of them are an 
item. When two people decide to maintain their relationship even though 
they live in different places, this is called a long-distance relationship. 
Two people who want to spend every 
minute of the day together can be described 
as inseparable. Some couples feel they are 
soul mates – connected to each other on the 
deepest level. When you want to comment 
that two people in a relationship are very 
compatible, you can say they’re a match 
made in heaven. 
Occasionally, two people have a relationship that seems completely wonderful 
and perfect, without any problems – this is called a fairy tale romance 
because it seems like a fantasy story!

Ending a Relationship 
If a couple breaks up or splits up, it means they separate. Sometimes the 
decision to break up is mutual – meaning both partners agree that it’s best to 
end the relationship. 
But if one person dumped the other, then it was only one 
person’s decision to end things, even though the other 
person wanted to continue. A person who gets dumped is 
often heartbroken – extremely sad. 
Relationship Habits 
Every relationship has its ups and downs (its good moments and bad 
moments), but here are some habits that can make a big difference. 
When getting into a new relationship, it’s important to recognize and deal 
with any baggage – that’s bad or complicated romantic experience in the past, 
which ends up having a negative effect on the new relationship. If the 
relationship consistently has problems, it can be described as a rocky or 
stormy relationship. 
 
 
A "rocky" or "stormy" relationship is full of conflict 
Some couples have a whirlwind romance – that’s a relationship that 
progresses extremely quickly to marriage. This sometimes works out – but 
often when a person is desperate to find a partner, they can rush the romance 
and end up ignoring red flags – those are signs of possible danger or

incompatibility in the relationship. Their partner might even turn out to be 
abusive – someone who physically, verbally, or emotionally injures another 
person. 
One of the secrets to a happy and healthy relationship is compromise – 
finding a fair solution in the middle of two different opinions, desires, or 
directions. After all, you won’t always see eye to eye (agree with each other 
or understand each other’s perspectives).  
Another expression for compromise is “give and take” – both partners need 
to work together, sometimes sacrificing their own wants, to preserve 
harmony in the relationship.
