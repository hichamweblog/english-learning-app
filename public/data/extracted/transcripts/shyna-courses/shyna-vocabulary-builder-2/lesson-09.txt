Lesson 9: Clothing & Fashion 
In this lesson, you’re going to learn three main vocabulary groups: words for 
various types of fabric, more advanced ways to describe the fit and form of 
clothing, and how to describe a person’s style. 
Fabrics 
Clothing can be made of various types of fabric – some are natural and others 
are synthetic (artificial): 
• cotton comes from a plant 
 
• silk is a very soft and luxurious fabric that comes from a worm  
 
 
• leather comes from the hide (skin) of an animal 


 
 
- EspressoEnglish.net 
 
• wool comes from sheep. The process of cutting the wool off the sheep is 
called “shearing.” 
 
• fur comes from animals – this is a fur coat 
 
• denim is the fabric used to make jeans 


 
 
- EspressoEnglish.net 
 
• lace is a fabric with decorative patterns 
 
• polyester, nylon, and spandex are synthetic fabrics. Polyester is used 
in many types of clothing and is often blended (mixed) with cotton; 
nylon is a strong and water-resistant material often used in jackets, 
tents, and backpacks; and spandex is an elastic material used in 
stretchy, close-fitting clothing.

Fit and Form 
You already know that clothing that is too small is tight, and clothing that is 
too big is loose – but now you’ll expand your vocabulary with some more 
descriptive words. 
Clothing that is extremely loose, with a lot of extra space, can be described as 
baggy. Clothing that follows the shape of your body more accurately, yet 
without being too tight, can be called tailored. And clothes that fit very close 


 
 
- EspressoEnglish.net 
to your body are form-fitting clothes. You can also say that the clothes fit you 
like a glove. 
Some people are too small or too large to fit into the typical sizes. Petite 
clothing is made for people who are short and slim, and plus-size clothing is 
for people who are larger than average. Maternity clothes are specially made 
for pregnant women.

Style 
Here are some words typically used to describe the style of clothing. 
Informal clothing for everyday wear is called casual, whereas clothes that are 
appropriate for “nicer” occasions – like going on a date or a job interview – are 
called dressy.  


 
 
- EspressoEnglish.net 
The adjective sporty describes clothes that have an athletic style, and the 
word trendy describes clothes that are modern and fashionable. Two other 
words for “trendy” are chic and stylish.   
If someone is wearing revealing clothing, it means the clothes show a 
significant portion of their body. The opposite, conservative clothing which 
covers most of your body, is described as modest clothing. 
A person who has no fashion sense (the natural ability to choose nice-looking 
clothes) might be dressed in clothing that is out of style (considered 
unattractive and unpopular nowadays) or that is not flattering – that means it 
doesn’t look good on their body.  
This person can hire a personal shopper who can help them revise their 
wardrobe – a word that can refer to all the clothing a person owns. The 
personal shopper can help you put together some nice outfits (the word 
“outfit” refers to the total combination of all the clothing and accessories you 
are wearing).
