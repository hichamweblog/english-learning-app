Don’t confuse its (possessive) with it’s (contraction for “it is”)! 
•
The cat ate it’s food 
•
The cat ate its food. 
•
Its illegal for a 17-year-old to buy alcohol. 
•
It’s illegal for a 17-year-old to buy alcohol.
