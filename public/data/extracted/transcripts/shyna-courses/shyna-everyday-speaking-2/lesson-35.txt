Lesson 35: A Bit of Gossip 
 
Caryn and Lucy are two high school students. Listen to them gossip about another girl 
they know, Jennifer, and mark the statements below “True” or “False.” 
 
Listening Comprehension 
 
True or false according to the dialogue? 
 
1. Jennifer was punished for fighting with Dan in the hallway 
2. Caryn and Lucy have seen Jennifer's new boyfriend before 
3. Jennifer's relationships tend to last a long time 
4. Caryn and Lucy frequently get asked out on dates 
5. Jennifer's boyfriends don't attend the same high school 
6. Jennifer was very upset after her last relationship ended 
7. Jennifer doesn't want Caryn and Lucy to date her ex-boyfriends 
8. Jennifer is on her way to lunch with her new boyfriend 
9. Caryn and Lucy don’t want to be friends with Jennifer anymore 
 
 


 
 
Conversation Text 
 
Lucy: So did you hear the latest about Jennifer? 
 
Caryn: About how she got detention for 
talking back to the choir teacher?  
 
Lucy: No, no, that’s old news. This is something 
even juicier. 
 
Caryn: Did she have another spat with Dan? 
 
Lucy: Close, but no cigar. 
 
Caryn: Well come on, don’t keep me in 
suspense!  
 
Lucy: OK, OK. Get a load of this – Jennifer’s 
still with Dan, but I heard from Vanessa that 
she’s also seeing that guy from Rockford High. 
You remember – the one from the football 
team. 
 
Caryn: You mean Jason? The one we were 
drooling over at the party on Saturday night? 
Yeah, of course I remember him! 
 
Lucy: That's the one. Apparently he has quite 
the reputation as a ladies’ man – and he’s got 
his own car. Vanessa says that he and Jennifer 
were making out in the park yesterday. 
 
Caryn: It’s so unfair! Jennifer gets all the hot 
guys. What’s her secret?

Lucy: Well, I have a hunch that her skimpy 
clothes probably have a lot to do with it. But 
it’s not like her relationships last… I mean, 
Dan is what, her third boyfriend so far this

she is. Meanwhile, nobody ever asks me out. 
 
Lucy: I’m in the same boat. Seems like it 
doesn’t matter how great of a person you

Caryn: So, Jennifer and Jason… could you 
imagine what would happen if Dan caught 
wind of this? 
 
Lucy: Jennifer’s probably doing everything 
in her power to make sure the two don’t 
cross paths. They do go to different schools, 
after all. 
 
Caryn: Yeah, but she’s not the sharpest

her. 
 
Lucy: Which is what she deserves. But then 
you and I will have to deal with the fallout. 
After her last breakup she was a basket 
case for a whole month. 
 
Caryn: Not to mention the fact that she’d 
give us the cold shoulder if we ever dared 
to date one of her exes.

We were just saying how great you looked at the

Caryn: Yeah and what a catch that guy from

Jennifer: Sort of. It’s complicated. Actually, I’m off

Lucy: OK, have fun!  
 
Caryn: Whew, that was close. Do you think she 
overheard us talking about her?  
 
Lucy: I don't think so – otherwise she would’ve 
said something to our faces. But we should 
probably be a little more careful next time. 
 
Caryn: I know, I would be mortified if she found 
out what we really think. If it weren't for Jennifer 
we would never be part of the popular crowd.  
 
Lucy: Our social lives would be over in a 
heartbeat.
