Lesson 19: The Times are Changing 
Noel is looking for a job, but he’s not having much success. His friend Tori tries to give 
him support and advice. Listen to their conversation and mark these statements as 
“True” or “False.” 
Listening Comprehension 
Are these statements true or false according to the dialogue? 
1. Noel dressed nicely for the interview. 
2. Tori was surprised to hear he wasn't hired. 
3. Noel thought it would be difficult to get the job. 
4. Noel was an excellent student. 
5. Mitch read Noel's resume carefully. 
6. Noel talked too much during the interview. 
7. Mitch rejected Noel because he didn't have enough experience. 
8. Tori is sympathetic to Noel's feelings. 
9. Tori's job also requires staying up-to-date. 
10. Tori offers to help Noel get a job at her company.

capable nurses?

job opportunities.
