Lesson 27: Bad Habits 
Mandy wants her boyfriend, Toby, to get rid of some bad habits. Listen to their 
conversation and try to answer these comprehension questions: 
1. Toby's bad habit is... 
a. biting his nails 
b. chewing on toothpicks 
c. picking his nose 
2. Mandy's bad habit is... 
a. cracking her knuckles 
b. chewing her lip 
c. playing with her hair 
3. Mandy wants him to stop because... 
a. he's going on a job interview 
b. her friends are coming to visit 
c. she wants to introduce Toby to her parents 
4. Toby works as a... 
a. construction worker 
b. farmer 
c. veterinarian 
5. Toby also makes a remark about Mandy's... 
a. eating habits 
b. hobbies 
c. whiny voice 
6. Toby says that his bad habit... 
a. has been with him since childhood 
b. is something practically everyone does 
c. isn't so bad when compared to others 
7. Toby suggests... 
a. making a deal to ignore each other's bad habits 
b. replacing their bad habits with conversation 
c. that bad habits are necessary to deal with stress. 
8. Mandy also wants to change Toby's... 
a. clothing 
 
b. haircut 
 
c. way of speaking 
 


 
 
Conversation Text 
Mandy: Will you stop biting your fingernails? It’s 
so gross. 
Toby: Hey, Valley Girl, this is Texas, not Los 
Angeles. Here in Texas a guy can bite his nails. 
Mandy: I’m not asking you to get a manicure – I 
just want my boyfriend to have decent-looking 
hands. Your fingernails have these ragged edges… 
they look like they’ve been gnawed on by a 
rodent.  
Toby: Doesn’t bother me. Besides, hands were 
made for workin’. Heck, ain’t no cow looking at my 
fingernails when I’m doing the milking. 
Mandy: We’re not on your farm, Toby, so get with 
the program. Next week my friends from LA are 
driving here to see me. If they see your nasty 
fingernails they’ll think you’re a slob. Like, for 
sure. 
Toby: Look, I’m a farmer, and darn proud of it. And 
if I want to bite my fingernails I will. It’s not like I 
chew my bottom lip like you do. 
Mandy: What’s that supposed to mean? 
Toby: Oh, come down off your high horse. You’re 
always biting your bottom lip like it’s a juicy steak 
or something. What’s the matter, the celery stick 
you ate for breakfast wasn’t enough for you? 
Mandy: Hey – I don’t wanna hear any of your snide 
comments about my weight!

Toby: All I’m saying is that you’ve got your 
vices too – so don’t fuss about mine. 
Mandy: You’re hopeless. Biting your nails 
is, like, a habit most people grow out of by 
the time they’re ten. It’s about time you 
stopped. 
Toby: It’s about time you stopped chewing 
on your lip. You’re doing it right now!  
Mandy: Okay! Fine – it’s a bad habit. But so 
is biting your nails.  
Toby: Yeah, but there are a lot of worse 
ones – like cussing and chewing tobacco 
and hitting the bottle, like my old man 
does. But I don’t do any of those. I just bite 
my fingernails when I get aggravated or 
stressed, that’s all.  
Mandy: That’s the same reason I chew on 
my lower lip. I don’t even realize I’m doing 
it. 
Toby: Okay, fine, then let’s make a deal. 
Whenever we feel the need to bite a 
fingernail or chew on a lip, we’ll just talk to 
each other instead. Deal? 
Mandy: Deal. I knew there was a reason I 
liked you. But you still need a makeover, 
farmer boy. I’ll never hear the end of it if 
my friends see you in those grubby 
overalls… 
Toby: Oh good grief, here we go again.

hitting the bottle = (slang) 
drinking alcohol

makeover = complete

I’ll never hear the end of it = I’ll
