Conversation Text 
Pat: How can I learn to speak Russian when I can’t 
even understand how to read it? And the wedding is 
just six months away. There’s no way, John… there’s 
just no way I can learn to speak Russian before then.  
John: You’re the one who’s marrying a Russian bride. 
But hey, chill out, Pat. The important thing is that you 
learn the basics. No one can really learn a language 
fluently in six months… not that I know of, at least. 
Pat: I know, but Veronica and I promised each other 
that we would learn each other’s language before our 
wedding. English isn’t exactly rocket science… but 
Russian sure seems like it. 
John: Hey, don’t knock the English language. How do 
you know Veronica isn’t having the same worries you 
are? I remember when I took classes to learn French… 
I wanted to learn the “romantic” language to sweep 
Tori off her feet. 
Pat: Yeah, I remember. You’re pretty fluent now, 
aren’t you?  
John: Yeah, but not at first. Pat, we’re from Georgia. 
When I first started to learn French I sounded like a 
drunken French hillbilly.
