Lesson 39: Music Lessons 
 
Charlie goes for his violin lesson with Mr. Lee. Listen to their conversation and mark 
the statements below “True” or “False.” 
 
Listening Comprehension 
True or false according to the dialogue? 
1. Charlie didn't practice this past week because his violin was broken 
2. This week was the first time he didn't practice 
3. The music lessons with Mr. Lee are free 
4. Charlie's parents want him to learn music 
5. Charlie enjoys playing sports 
6. The winners of some music competitions earn money 
7. Charlie thinks he could win a music competition 
8. Charlie likes heavy metal rock music 
9. Mr. Lee liked country music when he was a boy 
10. Charlie approves of Mr. Lee's proposal 
11. Mr. Lee cancels today's lesson and says he will see Charlie next week 
 
 


 
 
Conversation Text 
 
Mr. Lee: Hi Charlie, nice to see you! Have you been 
practicing? 
 
Charlie: Sorry, Mr. Lee. This week has been really 
packed. 
 
Mr. Lee: That’s what you said last week. 
 
Charlie: I know it sounds like a cop-out, but it’s 
true! I had a huge science project due on Friday, plus 
I had to mow the lawn and help fix my mom’s 
sewing machine.  
 
Mr. Lee: And the week before last, you said your dog 
ate your sheet music. Honestly, Charlie, getting you 
to practice is like pulling teeth. How can you expect 
to make any headway if you don’t practice? At this 
rate, you’ll never move on to more challenging 
pieces. 
 
Charlie: I can’t help it. I’m not trying to slack off… 
it’s just that after taking care of everything else, I 
can’t seem to muster the enthusiasm to practice.
