Lesson 06: The Perfect Puppy 
Will is trying to buy a dog and Susan is helping him at the pet shop. First, listen to their 
conversation and try to answer these comprehension questions: 
Listening Comprehension 
1. The problem with the first dog was... 
a. its age 
b. its color 
c. its size 
2. Will lives... 
a. in the city 
b. in the suburbs 
c. on a farm 
3. Will is going to give his son the dog on... 
a. Christmas 
b. his birthday 
c. the last day of school 
4. Will rejected the Dalmatian because... 
a. it could be aggressive 
b. it needs lots of open space 
c. it’s difficult to train 
5. He rejected the pug because... 
a. it's too small 
b. it tends to bark a lot 
c. it's ugly 
6. Will didn't buy any accessories because... 
a. he wanted his son to help choose them 
b. he's not sure what type of dog he'll get 
c. they're too expensive at the pet store 
7. What kind of pet does Will's wife want? 
a. a bird 
b. a cat 
c. a fish 
 


 
 
Conversation Text 
Will: Gee, I don’t know. I really wasn’t looking for a dog that size. My kid is seven years 
old. I’m not sure he can handle a big dog. 
Susan: A German shepherd is a very good dog. They’re 
smart and loyal and very protective. Also, this one’s still 
a puppy and not a full-grown adult yet. Your son and 
the German shepherd can grow up together. 
Will: Yeah, that’s true. But the problem is I live in a very 
close-knit suburban neighborhood. Eventually the 
German shepherd will get bigger… and big dogs bark. 
Even if my son could handle a dog that size, I’m not sure 
my neighborhood could. 
Susan: Okay, I understand your concerns. So let’s 
narrow it down to small dogs. How about… say… a 
Saint Bernard. 
Will: What?  
Susan: I was just pulling your leg.  
Will: Gee, you scared me. Listen, please, all I want is a 
little puppy… maybe a Chihuahua? 
Susan: Nah, guys aren’t Chihuahua type people, 
especially a seven year old. Kids have lots of energy.

Your son needs a puppy that can keep up with him – 
you know, run around, play fetch, and whatnot. 
Will: Oh great, just what I need: Two tornadoes 
running loose in my house. Why did my son have to ask

Susan: Calm down, dad, we’ll find the right puppy. 
Besides, turtles are boring, fish poop too much and 
lizards aren’t exactly cuddly. 
Will: Okay, what other kinds of puppies do you have?  
Susan: I was thinking a Dalmatian.  
Will: Still too big… and I’ve heard they’re aggressive – if 
he bit one of the neighbors, they’d sue me. 
Susan: Okay, so forget the Dalmatian. How about… a 
pug? They’re small and full of energy. 
Will: Nah, they’re ugly and their eyes give me the 
creeps. 
Susan: You’re not making this easy.  
Will: I’m sorry, but the puppy has to be just right. Don’t 
get me wrong, I love big dogs. I had a Georgia Blood 
Hound when I was a kid – great dog. But the fact is that 
my neighbors whine if the leaves fall off the trees too 
loudly.

Susan: That’s why I live on a farm – no neighbors

Will: I’m all ears.

They’re small, playful, full of energy and very

Will: Hey, yeah, I think that just might work. Do you 
have any here? 
Susan: A mother just gave birth to a litter of four. The 
puppies aren’t weaned yet. But they should be by the

Will: Terrific. Yeah, a Terrier is just right.  
Susan: Is there anything else I can help you with? Do 
you wanna buy a feeding dish, a collar, maybe some 
dog toys? 
Will: Not yet – I want to bring my son with me so he 
can pick them out. But there is one more thing – my 
wife wants a bird. Not too big and not too small. 
Susan: Here we go again.

Speaking Task 
Today there are two choices for the speaking task – please pick one! 
a. If you have (or used to have) a pet, tell me about it – your pet’s name, why you 
chose it, an interesting or funny story involving your pet 
b. What’s your opinion about animal rights? For example, should animals be 
kept in zoos / killed and eaten for food / experimented on for science? 
Explain your point of view and give reasons for your beliefs.
