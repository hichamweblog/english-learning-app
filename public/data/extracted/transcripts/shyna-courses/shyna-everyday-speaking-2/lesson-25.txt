Lesson 25: An Inspiring Teacher 
High-school students Nate and Ralph are talking about Mr. Smith, their favorite teacher. 
Listen to their conversation and answer these comprehension questions: 
Listening Comprehension 
1. Which subject does Mr. Smith teach? 
a. biology 
b. chemistry 
c. psychology 
2. Nate only passed the final because Mr. Smith... 
a. let him have extra time to finish 
b. tutored him before the exam 
c. was generous in his grading 
3. Ralph says that Mr. Smith's classes are... 
a. challenging 
b. easy 
c. fun 
4. Mr. Smith has been teaching for... 
a. 29 years 
b. 45 years 
c. 74 years 
5. If Nate hadn't passed the test, he would likely have to... 
a. go to an inferior college 
b. join the military 
c. work for his father 
6. What does Nate suggest doing for Mr. Smith? 
a. buying him a nice gift 
b. having a party 
c. writing a letter of appreciation 
7. Ralph suggests... 
a. nominating Mr. Smith for an award 
b. staying in touch with Mr. Smith 
c. taking another class with Mr. Smith next year 


 
 
Conversation Text 
Ralph: I thought I was going to flunk 
chemistry for sure. 
Nate: Ditto. I just barely squeaked by with a 
66 on the final. The saving grace was that Mr. 
Smith gave me partial credit for a few of the 
answers.  
Ralph: Mr. Smith is awesome. He really cares 
about his students. I can’t say the same about 
most of our teachers. 
Nate: I’m just thanking my lucky stars that I 
had Mr. Smith for chemistry, and not Ms. 
Johnson. She’s a stickler for perfection. 
Ralph: Or Mr. Potter. He just drones on 
without a break. But Mr. Smith always tries to 
spice things up with a joke or a game during 
class. 
Nate: Let me tell you, when I got my final 
grades, I breathed a huge sigh of relief. If I’d 
failed that exam, I could have lost my 
scholarship. But I didn’t, thanks to Mr. Smith. 
Ralph: And I was completely fed up with 
chemistry and ready to drop the class, but he 
went out of his way to help and encourage me.
