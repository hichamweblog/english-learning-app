Lesson 43: Surprise! 
 
Sarah is planning a surprise birthday party for her sister, Amy. She gets Amy’s 
boyfriend Jake to help. Listen to their conversation and answer these questions: 
 
Comprehension Questions 
1. Sarah told everyone to arrive at... 
a. 5:30 
b. 6:00 
c. 7:00 
2. Jake says that the guests... 
a. might ruin the surprise 
b. will probably be late 
c. won't remember to bring food 
3. Jake's job is to... 
a. cook dinner 
b. get Amy out of the house 
c. make the party decorations 
4. Jake describes Amy as... 
a. romantic 
b. shy 
c. stubborn 
5. Sarah is not sure that... 
a. Jake and Amy can afford the expense of a puppy 
b. Amy will be happy to get the puppy 
c. the puppy will behave itself during the party 
6. Sarah says the puppy has... 
a. been aggressive 
b. destroyed some of her stuff 
c. tried to run away 
7. Jake doesn't want to kidnap Amy or fake a medical emergency 
because... 
a. It would be too complicated to organize 
b. she might come back to the house too early 
c. she would be angry about the deception 
 


 
 
Conversation Text 
 
Jake: Okay, fill me in on the plan. 
 
Sarah: I told everyone to be here at 6 
o’clock sharp. 
 
Jake: Yeah… that’s wishful thinking. 
Nobody’s going to show up that soon 
after work. Especially with the traffic 
jams during rush hour. 
 
Sarah: Oh, I know. I said six because 
we’re really shooting for seven. I just 
need you to make sure Amy’s outta here 
by 5:30 so that I can get everything set 
up. 
 
Jake: And what if she doesn’t want to go 
anywhere? 
 
Sarah: Then don’t ask her to go, just 
whisk her away to a romantic birthday 
dinner with you. 
 
Jake: Come on, you know Amy. If I try to 
force her, she’ll dig her heels in. 
 
Sarah: Okay, then guilt her into it 
somehow. 
 
Jake: Guilt her? How am I supposed to 
do that? 
 
fill me in on = inform me 
about the details I am 
missing 
 
6 o’clock sharp = exactly at 
6:00 
 
wishful thinking = 
something you wish was 
true, but in reality is 
probably not going to 
happen 
 
show up = arrive, appear 
 
rush hour = the time when 
there’s a lot of traffic 
because people are going 
to/from work 
 
shooting for = aiming for, 
attempting 
 
whisk her away = take her 
away quickly 
 
dig her heels in = be 
stubborn and insist on doing 
what she wants 
 
guilt her = manipulate her by 
making her feel guilty or 
obligated to do something 
Vocabulary 


 
 
Sarah: Oh for crying out loud. I don’t know. 
Getting her out of the house is your job. I’ve 
done everything else. All the invitations, the 
catering, watching the puppy . . . 
 
Jake: Thanks for that, by the way. I owe you 
one. 
 
Sarah: I still have my doubts about this puppy 
thing. Are you absolutely sure she wants a 
puppy?  
 
Jake: Trust me. She wants the puppy. Who 
doesn’t love a surprise puppy? 
 
Sarah: I mean, that’s a lot of responsibility to 
just spring on a person. 
 
Jake: She’s dying to get a dog. She’s been 
nagging me about it for months, and I’ve been 
giving her the runaround.  
 
Sarah: What if it’s not the kind of dog she 
wants? 
 
Jake: She doesn’t care what kind of dog it is, she 
just wants something cute and furry. And this 
puppy is adorable. 
 
Sarah: Well, your adorable puppy chewed on 
my shoes and peed on my rug. 
 
Jake: Yeah, sorry about that. I’ll make it up to 
you, I promise.  
 
catering = 
professional food 
preparation/serving 
at a party or event 
 
I owe you one = I will 
do a favor for you in 
return for the favor 
you did for me 
 
spring on = surprise 
someone with 
 
nagging me = 
reminding me in a 
persistent, annoying 
way 
 
giving her the 
runaround = giving 
her excuses and 
answers that avoid 
the main issue or 
avoid making a 
commitment 
 
furry = full of fur 
(animal body hair)

I’ll make it up to you 
= I’ll compensate for 
the problem/damage 
Vocabulary 


 
 
Sarah: Just… make sure she’s not here from 
five-thirty to seven. Whatever you have to do. 
Kidnap her. Fake a medical emergency. Get 
yourself arrested and call her to bail you out. 
Whatever. 
 
Jake: Hmm, those ideas would probably 
backfire. She’d be mad if she found out I lied to 
her. How about I just take her to dinner? 
 
Sarah: Yes! Exactly. Stop overthinking it; she’ll 
be thrilled if you wine and dine her.  
 
Jake: Okay. I’ll text you when we’re on our way 
home from dinner. Anything else? 
 
Sarah: That’s it. You distract Amy for a couple of 
hours, and I’ll take care of the rest. 
 
Jake: All right. She’s going to be blown away by 
this surprise party – I can’t wait!

against their will
