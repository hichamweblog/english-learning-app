Lesson 30: Losing Your Temper 
 
Listen to this conversation between Jack and his wife Lynn, who lost her temper 
at work. Mark each sentence as “True” or “False.”  
 
Listening Comprehension 
 
True or false according to the dialogue? 
 
1. One of Lynn's colleagues didn't come to work 
2. The restaurant was busy the entire night 
3. The restaurant was featured in a tourist guide 
4. Lynn had to answer a lot of questions  
5. Lynn's boss helped her with the customers 
6. There were children in the group of tourists 
7. The customers were rude to Lynn 
8. Lynn took a break after everyone left 
9. Her boss was angry because Lynn didn't serve everyone fast enough 
10. Lynn was fired from her job

the situation? Have you ever “lost your temper”? (lost control of your 
words/actions in anger). Alternatively, you can talk about a time someone 
else was angry with you.
