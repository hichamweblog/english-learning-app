Lesson 44: An Argument 
 
Listen to this argument between two roommates, Nadia and Elizabeth, and choose the 
best answer to each comprehension question: 
 
Listening Comprehension 
1. Nadia isn't feeling well because she has... 
a. a hangover 
b. a headache 
c. the flu 
2. Elizabeth was woken up by... 
a. loud music from the living room 
b. Nadia making too much noise 
c. the strange girl on the couch 
3. In the future, Elizabeth wants Nadia... 
a. to clean up the mess she makes 
b. not to stay out so late 
c. to pay more for the extra food she eats 
4. Elizabeth is anxious because... 
a. Nadia appears to be very sick 
b. her boyfriend is coming for lunch 
c. the kitchen is dangerous 
5. Nadia's friend Alice also... 
a. lost Elizabeth's book 
b. ruined Elizabeth's book 
c. stole Elizabeth's book 
6. Nadia promises to... 
a. ask her friends to behave better 
b. get the bookshelf fixed 
c. replace Elizabeth's book 
7. Elizabeth and Nadia agree that... 
a. their friends don't get along with each other 
b. they can't stand living together 
c. they need to take better care of the apartment
