Welcome to the  
English Idioms Course! 
 
Before we begin, I want to teach you the method we will use to help you get 
the most benefit out of the lessons. Each lesson will have four parts: 
1) Introductory Quiz – You will study the idioms in context and guess the 
correct meaning. This helps you think ACTIVELY about the idioms and 
develop the skill of understanding them from the context. 
 
2) Explanation – Learn more details about the meaning and use of the 
idioms, with examples of situations when they can be used. 
 
3) Practice Quiz – Put the idioms into practice by choosing the correct 
idiom to complete each sentence. 
 
4) Use the idioms in your own English – Each lesson includes several 
short-answer exercises. The questions are designed to help you use the 
idioms naturally in your own words. If you want feedback or correction, 
you can e-mail your sentences to me. 
 
This method helps you learn better because you work intensely with each 
idiom or expression – not just try to memorize it. Let’s get started!
