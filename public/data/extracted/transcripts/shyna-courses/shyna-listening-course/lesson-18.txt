Lesson 18: Parents 
Warm-Up: 
Write a bit about your parents. What kind of 
work/activities do they like to do? What are their 
personalities like? Are you more similar to your 
mother or your father? 
 
 
 
 
Activity 1 – Pre-Learn Vocabulary 
outgoing (adj.) = extroverted; a person who loves social situations 
Ex) People who are outgoing can walk into a party where they don’t know 
anybody and make a dozen friends by the end of the night. 
gifted (adj.) = describing a person as “gifted” means they have special natural 
intelligence or talent 
Ex) She’s a very gifted writer – her first book was published when she was just 
14 years old. 
Navy (n.) = the branch of the military forces that involves ships, submarines, and 
other operations in the ocean 
 
combat (n.) = fighting, battle 
 
Ex) My grandfather fought in World War II and was injured in combat. 


MBA (n.) = Master of Business Administration – an advanced university degree in 
business 
repertoire (n.) = the range of skills or accomplishments of a particular person or 
group. Often used for the set of songs that a musician or musical group can play. 
Ex) I play mostly rock music, but I’d like to add some jazz songs to my 
repertoire.

Complete Transcript 
Today I’m going to tell you a little bit about my parents. I’ll start with my mom. She 
is one of the most energetic people you’ll probably ever meet. She is extremely 
outgoing, the type of person who can strike up a conversation with absolutely 
anyone, and if you get her talking on one of the topics she loves – like fitness or 
music – she might just talk your ear off! 
My mom is really into exercise and healthy living – but that wasn’t always the case. 
She was overweight for many years, but over the past seven years or so, she’s lost a 
ton of weight and completely changed her lifestyle. Now she’s passionate about 
nutrition and she works out six days a week. One of her favorite workouts is called 
Zumba, which is a mixture of different Latin dance forms that’s used for exercise; 
it’s all the rage in the U.S. nowadays.  
Her other passion is music. When my brother and I were little, she worked as a 
piano teacher and students would come to our house for their lessons. She also 
taught me and my brother to play. Of course we couldn’t get away with not 
practicing because we lived with our piano teacher! Nowadays she sings in two 
choirs that perform both locally and internationally – they’ve had concerts in 
Europe, Australia and New Zealand, and they’re planning one in South America. 
My mom worked for many years as a math teacher – not in a regular classroom, but 
with groups of gifted kids who needed more of a challenge than the math they were 
getting in their grade. So she would come up with games and creative ways to teach 
these advanced math concepts. Later she also worked with kids who were 
struggling in math as well, and used these same creative teaching techniques to help 
make math more fun and easier to understand for kids who were having a hard 
time with it. 
Before my brother and I were born, my mom served in the Navy. She entered a Navy 
scholarship program when she was in college, and was actually one of the first 
women in that program. After graduating, she stayed in the Navy for two years, 
working in the area of supplies – coordinating the purchase and distribution, that 
type of thing. 
My dad was also a Navy officer, and he served on a nuclear submarine, although he 
was never in combat. After leaving the Navy he worked as an engineer, his specialty 
was optical engineering and he actually worked on the Hubble Space Telescope, 


which is one of the largest telescopes in orbit around the earth. Later in his career 
he got his MBA and switched from engineering to finance.  
The last company he worked for shut down its local branch a few years ago, and 
luckily my dad had enough money saved up to retire, because otherwise it would’ve 
been hard for him to find another job at 58. So he retired early, and now he does 
some volunteer work for a non-profit organization; he helps them with their 
finances, strategy, and general management. 
My dad is quieter and more reserved than my mom, but he’s still very friendly and 
approachable. He also plays a musical instrument – the guitar – specifically, classical 
guitar. He used to play baseball, tennis, and volleyball, but then he had two knee 
surgeries and the doctor told him to take up a sport that wouldn’t be so hard on his 
joints – so he started ice skating. Now he skates every week; he has a coach and he’s 
even participated in competitions. 
Another somewhat surprising ability my dad has is making Japanese food. He 
taught himself how to make some basic sushi many years ago, and then started 
getting more and more complex and creative with various kinds of fish and 
vegetables… and he’s also expanded his repertoire into Japanese soups and 
appetizers. I keep telling him he should open a restaurant or something, but he just 
likes to cook for family and friends. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 


Extra Vocabulary 
"she might just talk your ear off!" 
 
This expression means “talk too much” or “talk without stopping.” 
"it’s all the rage in the U.S. nowadays." 
 
This expression means something is currently very popular/fashionable. 
"one of the largest telescopes in orbit around the earth" 
 
“In orbit” means something is in space, going in circles around the earth. 
"The last company he worked for shut down its local branch a few years ago" 
In this context, the word “branch” means an office of a company that is located 
in a particular region.
