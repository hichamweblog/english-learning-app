Lesson 14 – About My Pets 
Have you ever had a pet? If so, write about it.  
If not, what type of pet would you like to have? 
 
 
 
Activity 1 – Pre-Learn Vocabulary 
auction (n.) = a public sale in which each item is sold to the person who offers the 
highest price for it. The various offers of prices at an auction are called “bids.” 
Ex) I bid $200 for an antique table at an auction, but I didn’t get it because 
someone else bid $350. 
autistic (adj.) = a person who is autistic has the medical condition called autism, 
which seriously limits the person’s ability to communicate and interact socially 
Ex) iPads can sometimes help autistic kids communicate through pictures and 
symbols. 
fixed (adj.) = in the context of this audio, a pet being “fixed” means it has been 
neutered; it has had an operation on its sexual organs so that it can’t reproduce 
Ex) Our dog had eight puppies and we had to give them all up for adoption. The 
following month, we had her fixed so that this won’t happen again. 
litter (n.) = in the context of this audio, “litter” refers to an absorbent material that 
is used inside an animal’s cage or box (called a “litter box”), which can absorb the 
animal’s urine/feces: 
 


flea / tick (n.) = both of these are tiny insects that drink blood and are often found 
on cats and dogs 
 
flea   
 
 tick 
mischief (n.) = this word refers to trouble or behavior that causes annoyance or a 
disturbance – but usually of a fun and innocent type 
Ex) My son got into some mischief at school – he put a rubber snake on the 
teacher’s chair. 
fond (adj.) = if you are “fond of” someone or something, it means you have a strong 
liking for it, you feel affection for it 
Ex) I’m very fond of that author’s writing; I’ve read all her books and loved 
every single one.

ADAM 
I wanted to tell you about my pet. My pet's name is Nightshadow. It's a little cat, it's 
four months old, and it got the name because my son liked the color, the color is 
totally black, and he wanted to name it "Night," and we used to have a dog named 
"Shadow," so I said hey, let's call it Nightshadow. He agreed and he loved that. 
I have two little boys and we wanted a pet in the house and so we went ahead and 
we got this cat. Now the way we got the cat was really nice. There's an auction site 
that I go to and I buy things from. The lady who worked there put a cat up on sale

So I said "Hey, we're looking for a pet," she gave me information, and we talked for

So she came over, brought all the items - it was really cool too because the cat was 
not only fixed, it had a chip inside of it and it’s got our address, she made sure it

So it's a really great pet, she’s so much fun, she climbs, she even jumps up on my

CHRISTINA 
Hi, my name is Christina and I want to tell you about some of the animals that I have 
at my house. The only animal that we have living indoors with us is a white 


Labrador puppy, and her name is Izzy. We've had her for about a month now, and

I usually get up about six AM and I feed her and take her outside to go to the

Izzy is really, really good with kids, she really likes them a lot, and she loves to kiss

Whenever he is crying, she is always going over to him to try to take care of him - 
but she doesn't realize how big she is, and so usually we have to pull her back so

Since I've only had her a month, I can't really say that she's been in a whole lot of

with water and my boys made their way over there and started splashing in the 
water. 
Well, they had their raincoats on and their rain boots on, so that wasn't so big of a

been a real blessing in our house and we really love her very very much. 
I also have some chickens, and the chickens are pretty easy to take care of. We go 
outside in the morning, usually when I'm taking my dog out, and I'll let them out of

I also have a rabbit that my oldest got when he was two years old, and so of course

Extra Vocabulary 
"It needed a good home because it was a rescue cat" 
 
This means the cat was rescued from living on the streets. 
Labrador puppy (n.) = a “puppy” is a baby dog; and “Labrador” is the name of the 
dog’s breed (type): 
 
“we spend about five minutes running around outside, chasing each other and 
tossing a ball” 
 
The verb “toss” means to throw something lightly and casually. 
"between the road and the hill is a small drainage area for water to go into the 
creek." 
A creek is a very, very small river. The word “drainage” refers to excess water 
leaving an area and going out into another place. 
 "my boys started splashing in the water" 
The word “splash” means to make movements in the 
water so that water flies everywhere. 
"I'll let them out of their chicken coop" 
 
A “chicken coop” is a place to keep chickens.
