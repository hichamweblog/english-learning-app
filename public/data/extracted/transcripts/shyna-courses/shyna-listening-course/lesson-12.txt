Complete Transcript 
Hi, my name is Chris and I wanted to tell you about some embarrassing moments 
that I had from my childhood. 
The first one isn't that big of a deal, but I still remember being embarrassed. I 
wanted to go get some candy, and I didn't have enough change. Well, I noticed that 
my brother had some sitting on his nightstand - and so I figured I'd borrow a little 
bit. Well later on, I found out that I accidentally spent his two-headed nickel, and he 
was really, really upset – and I was so embarrassed because I couldn't do anything 
about it.  
The next embarrassing moment happened when I was about three or four years old. 
My family took us to a roller coaster park called King’s Island. Now, during the day I 
had to go to the bathroom. Well, I did what a lot of three or four-year-old boys do: I 
pulled my pants and underwear down to my ankles, and I was ready to go right 
where I was standing. Fortunately, my parents were able to stop me before I did 
anything. Unfortunately, during this time a couple of girls walked by, and they 
started laughing at my bare bottom. Looking back, they were probably just laughing 
because it was cute, but I remember being so embarrassed as a child. 
This one is probably the most embarrassing moment that I can recall. My uncle 
Ronnie took me to a friend's farm. They invited us over for dinner and said that I 
could explore their farm before we ate. So I went out and I checked things out. I 
ended up going inside of one of the barns. I got to see some cats, some pigs and 
some cows. Well after a while my uncle called for me, saying it was time for dinner. 
So I headed out of the barn, but before I made it to the door, I noticed this pit - it was 
about two feet wide, one foot deep, and spanned the length of the barn - so I jumped 
across it.  
The problem is, I didn't make it - I fell down inside of the pit. And I noticed it was 
pretty sticky and gooey and yucky, and it smelled really bad. I cried out for some 
help; my uncle rushed in and discovered that I was covered from head to toe in cow 
poop. He took me back to the house, and I remember standing there on their porch 
with the entire family looking at me, and I was just covered. It was so embarrassing! 
But luckily they were so gracious and kind; they let me use their bathroom, and I 
got a shower and got cleaned up. And they even still served me the best dinner that 
I ever had. 


So my advice to you is if you ever find yourself in that same situation, make sure you 
can jump the entire pit, or don't bother trying at all. 
 
Extra Vocabulary 
"The first one isn't that big of a deal" 
If something is “a big deal” it means it’s very important, very significant – so 
something that isn’t a big deal means it’s not very important/significant. 
"I accidentally spent his two-headed nickel" 
The two sides of a coin are called heads and tails. 
A “two-headed nickel” means a 5-cent coin on 
which both sides have the “heads” image. It would 
be an unusual item, a collector’s item. 
"the pit… spanned the length of the barn" 
 
This means that the pit extended throughout the length of the barn. 
"it was pretty sticky and gooey and yucky" 
“Sticky” describes a substance that is adhesive. Tape and glue are sticky. The 
adjective “gooey” refers to a thick liquid that is also sticky. Honey and 
chocolate sauce can be described as gooey. 
"But luckily they were so gracious and kind" 
If a person is “gracious,” it means he/she is nice, polite, friendly, and 
compassionate. This is different from “graceful,” which describes a person 
who is elegant in his/her movements (a dancer is graceful).
