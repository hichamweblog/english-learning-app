enice came into being after the Lombard hordes invaded

Northern Italy in AD 568. The Romans who lived in the
cities of this area retreated onto hundreds of small islands that
lay just off the coast of the Adriatic Sea. They built their houses
and other buildings on wooden beams called “pilings.” To
connect the islands, they built hundreds of bridges. Most of
these bridges are arched so that boats can pass under them. As

a result, many of the “streets” of Venice are actually waterways.

The commercial spirit of Venice lives on today in the busy
Rialto market. There, tourists can find all sorts of goods on sale.
