Liverpool, but rather Bordeaux, France. So Fogg
paid the captain 2,000 pounds for each passenger
to travel with him to Liverpool.

take a train to Dublin and then a fast boat to
Liverpool, thereby gaining a few hours.

Twenty minutes before noon on December 2lst,

dock. They were only six hours away from
London. Suddenly, Fogg felt a end on his shoul-
der. It was Mr. Fix.

“Phileas Fogg, I arrest you in the Queen’s name,”
said Mr, Fix.

From the station, Fogg ordered Passepartout to
