CHAPTER ONE

Montego High School

ontego Bay is a beautiful tourist town on the

east coast of Florida, near Miami Beach. The

town was founded in the seventeenth century

by the pirates of the Caribbean Sea, who were
called “buccaneers”. Montego Bay became their home port, and
there are still legends about hidden treasure there.

The new Montego Bay Wax! Museum opened recently. It is a
big tourist attraction with wax statues of famous explorers,
buccaneers, sea captains, slave merchants? and criminals.

Our story starts one Friday morning in October at the
Martins’ home. Bill and Michelle Martin lived with their parents
in a nice old house.

Bill was a tall boy of sixteen, with brown hair and blue eyes.

1. Wax: candles are made from this.

2. slave merchants : men who bought and sold slaves between the 16th
and 19th centuries.
