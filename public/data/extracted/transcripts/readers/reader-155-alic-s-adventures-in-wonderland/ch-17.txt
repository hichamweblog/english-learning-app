What is a limerick?

It’s a short, funny poem which rhymes.

There was an old person of Chili

Whose conduct was painful and silly;

He sat on stairs

Eating apples and pears

That imprudent old person of Chili
Chili, silly, stairs, pears have similar sounds. They rhyme. If we put them together we
have a limerick.
