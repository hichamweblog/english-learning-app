our two-week trip on the River Thames.

Harris was wearing a red and orange blazer.! It was a

When we passed Hampton Court Palace, * Harris asked, ‘Have
you ever visited the maze? here?’ He said he had gone into the

Harris said to his friend, ‘We'll go in and walk around for ten

In the maze, they met some people who had been there for

afraid. He continued turning to the right, but he was still in the
maze.

Harris got lost! The people were angry with him. They all
