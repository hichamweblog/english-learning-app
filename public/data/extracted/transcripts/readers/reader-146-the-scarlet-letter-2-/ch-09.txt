Most of those watching said that they saw a scarlet letter,
like Hester Prynne's, on the priest's chest. But how long had
it been there? There were various explanations, all of which
were no more than guesses. Some said that Mr Dimmesdale

Hester Prynne received ber scarlet letter. Others said that old
Roger Chillingworth had caused it to appear, by some devilish
magic. And others said it was a terrible sign of a guilty heart,
and of God's punishment. The reader may choose from these
possible explanations.
