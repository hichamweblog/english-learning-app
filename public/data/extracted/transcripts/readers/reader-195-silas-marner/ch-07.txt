He had been a much happier man since that fateful New Year's
Eve. Dunstan had never come home. Everyone thought that he
had gone to the colonies and would never return. Gradually
Godfrey stopped worrying that any day Dunstan might come back
and reveal the secrets of his past life. For the first time in years,
he felt free again. Now he could marry Nancy and his life would
be paradise. He imagined a future in which Nancy smiled at him
as he played with their children by the hearth in the big red
house.

But he would not forget his other child. That was a father's
duty.
