their own position in society and snobbish towards anyone they

important it was to them for Lady Dalrymple to recognise them
and treat them as equals. Lady Dalrymple was pleasant enough,
but she was nothing special. She was not intelligent or cultured.
Certainly she smiled at everyone, but the only reason Elizabeth
called her ‘charming’ was because she was a wealthy aristocrat.

that Sir Walter has found good company in Bath.’
