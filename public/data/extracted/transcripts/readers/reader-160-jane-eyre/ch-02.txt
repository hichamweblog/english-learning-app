I stayed with Helen through the night to comfort her, and in
the morning I found that she had died.

As a result of so many pupils dying at the school, there was
an inquiry 4 into the conditions which had caused the disease.

light clothing which the children were given, they gave money
to improve the lives of the girls. Lowood School was a much
