We were all silent as we walked from the church back to

too, was the frightening, terrible woman that I had seen in
my bedroom. She was the person who had the cruel laugh.
She was the one who had set fire to Mr Rochester’s bed, who
had tried to kill Mr Mason, and who had ruined my veil. Yes,
she was mad, but she was also Mr Rochester’s wife. I knew
that I could not marry him.

Although I felt sorry for Mr Rochester, I knew that I must
leave my home, Thornfield Hall, for ever. I put a few clothes
into a small bag. I took a little money, and quietly left
Thornfield Hall early the next morning. I told no one that I

was going, and no one saw me leave.
