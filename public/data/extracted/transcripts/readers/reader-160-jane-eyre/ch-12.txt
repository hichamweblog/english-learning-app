I lay awake ! in my bed that night, and I thought about St
John. I could not decide what I should do. I knew that I did not
love him, and I was sure that he did not love me. But maybe I
should go to India? The night was very quiet. I could hear
nothing in the darkness.

Suddenly, I thought that I heard a voice. ‘Jane!’ it called,
‘Jane! Jane!’

It was Mr Rochester’s voice.

‘Iam here, Mr Rochester.’ I cried. ‘Where are you? What is
wrong?’

Was I dreaming? Perhaps, but it didn’t matter. Somehow, I
knew that Mr Rochester needed me. ‘I must go to him at once,’ 2

The next day, I left once more for Thornfield Hall. It was a
