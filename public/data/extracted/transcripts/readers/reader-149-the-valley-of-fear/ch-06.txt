We use relative clauses to give extra information about something
without starting a separate sentence. A relative clause often begins
with'the words who, what, where, when, which or that. We can use
these instead of repeating certain words. Sometimes the information in
this clause is interesting but not essential to our understanding. This is
called a non-defining relative clause.
