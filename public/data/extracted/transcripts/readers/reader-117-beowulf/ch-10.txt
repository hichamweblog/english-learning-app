It seems possible that Anglo-Saxon swords were capable of cutting
through armour, although modern tests do not support this theory
entirely. It is possible that the heavy weight of the blade was used to
break bones and crush internal organs. A number of skeletons found
in ancient graves from northern Europe show just how dangerous a
sword could be even when a man was protected by armour. There is
evidence of terrible injuries, although in some cases the bone has
grown back, meaning that the man continued to live even after such a
horrific injury.
