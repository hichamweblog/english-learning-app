By the light of the moon, | gazed at that pale
forehead, those closed eyes, those locks of hair
trembling in the wind, and I said to myself, “What
I'm looking at is only a shell. What’s most
important is invisible. . .”

As his lips parted in a half smile, I said to myself,
again, “What moves me so deeply about this
sleeping little prince is his loyalty to a flower — the
image of a rose shining within him like the flame
within a lamp, even when he’s asleep. . .” And I
realized he was even more fragile than I had
thought. Lamps must be protected: a gust of wind
can blow them out... .

And walking on like that, I found the well at

daybreak.
