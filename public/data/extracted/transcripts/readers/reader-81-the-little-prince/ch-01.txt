I ask the indulgence of the children

who may read this book for dedicating it

to a grown-up. I have a serious reason: he is the
best friend I have in the world. I have another
reason: this grown-up understands everything, even
books about children. I have a third reason; he lives
in France where he is hungiy and cold, He needs
cheering up. If all these reasons are not enough, |
will dedicate the book to the child from whom this
grown-up grew. All grown-ups were once children —
although few of them remember it. And so | correct
my dedication:
