Rob looked at this other man closely. He was a

Just then, the horn blew to begin the last round.
The archers took their places. Each took his turn
with some hesitation because the target was far
away. None of them put an arrow into the inner
circle. Then it was Rob's turn. He also hesitated.
His eyes moved to Maid Marian’s booth. She met
his look and smiled. At that moment, Rob knew
she recognized him despite his disguise. He knew
he would win the prize for her and gain back
some of his family’s honor. With new confidence,
he drew back his bow and launched his arrow

straight and true into the center of the target.
