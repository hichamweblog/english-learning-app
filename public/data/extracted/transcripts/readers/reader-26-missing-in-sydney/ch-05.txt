From the Manly area of Sydney, the 10 km ‘Manly Scenic Walkway’
takes you past some smaller and quieter beaches. As the name |
suggests, there are a lot of panoramic views.

It takes three and a half hours to walk the 10 kilometres, but most
people take a lot longer because they stop to swim in the sea on the |
way. |
When you arrive at the end, there is a boat that takes you back to
Circular Quay. If you catch the boat in the early evening, you may see |
the sun setting ! over Sydney Opera House as you arrive back.

The perfect end to a perfect day.
