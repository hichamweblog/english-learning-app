he Southern Lake District is most famous for the poets and

artists who lived there. There are museums and historic Sites to

visit here with information about their interesting lives.
The Wordsworth Museum and Dove Cottage, for example, are two
very popular tourist attractions. They are the former homes of
William Wordsworth and Thomas de Quincey. In the summer there
are poetry.readings and talks about the work of these two men.
William Wordsworth (1790-1850) is one of the
most famous British poets. He was a Romantic

poet, famous for using an emotional style in his

poems which were usually about Nature.

He wrote “The Prelude”, “Tintern Abbey” and

“Daffodils.” He didn’t write in his house. He said
that poets should write outside to feel nearer to
Nature, even in the rain! Wordsworth influenced
Keats, Shelley, Byron and many other famous poets.

John Ruskin (1819-1900) was a very famous art critic and philosopher
who influenced traditional opinions about painting. He spent a lot of
time walking in the Lake District drawing the landscape. He wrote
the book “Modern Painters”.

Thomas de Quincey (1785-1859) was a journalist and essayist.
He wrote about many controversial subjects in a new style influenced
by dreams and the sub-conscious. His most famous book is “Confessions
of an Opium Eater”, the story of his life, which he wrote in 1822.
His work influenced the American writer Edgar Allan Poe and the
French poet Baudelaire.
