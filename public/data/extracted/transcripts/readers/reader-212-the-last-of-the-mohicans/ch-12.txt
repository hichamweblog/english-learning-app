Say and tell

We can use both say and tell as reporting verbs. If we mention
who we’re talking to, we usually use tell + the indirect object.
We don’t put to before the indirect object.

If we don’t mention who we're talking to, we use say. Look at
these sentences:

James told me that you were ill.
