Past Perfect versus Past Simple

Look at this sentence from Chapter 1:

It had been their home for centuries.

The Past Perfect tense is used when we are already talking about the
past and talk about a situation that occurred before that time. Look at

this example:

I knew New York very well. I had lived there for eight years.
