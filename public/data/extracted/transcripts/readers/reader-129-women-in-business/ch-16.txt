Chapter 5
Students work in pairs or small groups. They are
'paparazzi' – photographers who earn a living by selling
photographs of famous people, like Madonna and Sean
Penn, often without permission. Students discuss and
write down reasons why they should be allowed to take
any photographs they like and sell them to newspapers.
Compare reasons with the whole class.
