6 Writing
A Study in Scarlet is written in the first person. The narrator simply
tells us the events as they happen. Write your own account of the
events that have happened to you in the past week. You can make it
more personal and write your own feelings about the events if you
want, or you can simply write about the most important details and
how they happened. You choose!
