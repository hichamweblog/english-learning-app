The next morning, John woke up to find a note on his bed. It
said, ‘Twenty nine days left.’ John did not know how this message
was delivered as he slept. All the doors and windows were locked.

Every day John found another note, Counting down the days
until Lucy had to choose. There was still No news from Jefferson.

One evening John sat alone trying to think of a way out of his
trouble. That morning the number two Was written on the wall of
his house. The next day was the last day of Lucy’s freedom.
Suddenly he heard a quiet scratching on the door to the house.
John opened the door. He looked right and left and saw no one.
Then he looked down; there on the ground was Jefferson Hope.

“You scared me!’ said John. ‘Why did you come like that?’
