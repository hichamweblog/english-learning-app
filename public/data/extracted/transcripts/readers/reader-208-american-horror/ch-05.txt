Look at these sentences:

He painted the woman who loved him.

That is the book which tells the story of the painting.
These are the paintings that fascinated Edgar Allan Poe.
He is the man that told me about Italy.

That is the hotel where we stayed last summer.

They all contain defining relative clauses. A defining relative clause is
essential to the sentence: without it the sentence is not complete.

Notice that we use:

¢ who for people

¢ which for animals and things

¢ that for people, animals and things
¢ where for places

In spoken English that is generally used instead of which.
