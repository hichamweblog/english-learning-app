‘| want you to clean the shelves’

We can use the object and to + infinitive after verbs like want, need,
would like, tell, ask, and get, when we're talking about people giving
orders or causing other people to do things. Usually we write negative
sentences of this type like this: | don’t want you to clean the shelves.
With tell and ask, you can also say / told you not to clean the shelves and
the meaning is a bit different. Can you find another sentence like this in
Chapter Four? Look at the way Ellie’s father talks to her about
delivering the newspapers.
