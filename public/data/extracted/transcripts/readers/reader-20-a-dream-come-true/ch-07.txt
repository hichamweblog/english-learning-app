2) Dry stone walls

In Yorkshire, and in many parts of Britain, dry stone walls are used
to separate fields; many of them are hundreds of years old. But in
some places in the world you can see enormous dry stone walls
used in ancient cities. Just one famous example is Machu Picchu in
Peru, but there are many others.

1 Are there dry stone walls in your country? If so, where? What
were they used for?

2 Use reference books or the Internet to find out about famous
examples of dry stone constructions in the world.
