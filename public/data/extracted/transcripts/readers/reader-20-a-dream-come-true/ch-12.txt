We can use say or tell to report what someone has said.

Liz said, ‘Ellie, you are going to win!’

Liz told Ellie (that) she was going to win.

Liz said (that) Ellie was going to win.

In the second and third sentence, speech marks (‘’) are not used, and
the verb to be changes from the present to the past tense.

After say you mustn’t use an indirect object: He said he was happy
NOT Heseidnehewashapey.

After tell you must use an indirect object: He told me he was happy

NOT Hetetd fewastappy.
