The Rise and Fall

of Chivalry

he idea of chivalry is closely associated with the rise of
i the medieval knight. With his heavy armor, lance, and
sword, the knight had become the most awesome force on the
battlefield, Some scholars claim that chivalry evolved from the
German tribes in which the bravest warriors were chosen to be
companions and guardians of their king. However, these early
German knights did not believe in defending the weak, which
was one of the main ideals of chivalry. It wasn’t until the 11th
century that the code of chivalry was established: “to speak the
truth, to help the oppressed and weak, and to never turn back
from the enemy.” This code quickly spread throughout

Europe.
