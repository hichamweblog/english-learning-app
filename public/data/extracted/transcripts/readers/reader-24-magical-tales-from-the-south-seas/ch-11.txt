There are two beautiful national parks: Haleakala National Park on

The first Hawaiians were Polynesians from the South Pacific, but
today there are many different ethnic groups in Hawaii. More than
half of Hawaii’s population have Japanese, Chinese, Filipino or
Korean origins. During the late 1800s and in the 1900s many Asians
went to work in the pineapple and sugar plantations and settled
there. About a quarter of the population is white or part white, and

the others are of Hawaiian origin.
