Unidentified Flying Objects, or UFOs, are often sighted by people all

over the world. About fifty sightings are reported every day. These
mysterious flying objects are sometimes photographed and filmed.
People talk about them a lot. Some people say they visited aliens on
UFOs! Most of the pictures and films of UFO are fakes. ! And others
have a scientific explanation. Every year there are international
meetings where ufologists 2 study and discuss the latest sightings
and other UFO news.

Do UFOs really exist?

There are many explanations for bright lights and objects with
strange shapes that appear in the sky. They can be bright airplane
lights, secret military airplanes, satellites, weather balloons or

strange cloud shapes. Or perhaps they are UFOs!
