Death and Burial at Stratford

hakespeare retired from the theatre in 1610, as his health was not

good, and returned to Stratford and his family. In 1613 the Globe
Theatre burned down and he lost a lot of money, but still seemed to
have been quite wealthy as he was able to share in the financing
of The New Globe some months later. On April 23, 1616, Shakespeare
died at the age of 52. He was jburiedin the Church of the Holy Trinity
in Stratford. Today the house where he lived in Stratford is a museum
and The Globe Theatre, which he worked so hard to make successful,
has recently been rebuilt in London as a permanent venue for his works
and a memorial to Britain’s most famous writer.

courtyard. In this courtyard a temporary stage could be erected opposite

the main entrance. The spectators stood in the yard on three sides

of the stage, and those who couldgafford to pay the innkeeper

had the privilege of sitting in the balconiesgeverlooking the courtyard.
The old type of inn-yard was kept as the model when new theatres

were built. The theatres were open to the sky, and were square,
