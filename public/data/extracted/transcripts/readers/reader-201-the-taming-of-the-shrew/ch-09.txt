Marriage and Life in London

n 1582, when he was 18, he married Anne Hathaway, a Stratford girl.
Anne was seven or eight years older than him. Their first daughter,
Susanna, was born in 1583, and in 1585 twins, a boy and a girl,
Hamnet and Judith, were born. What Shakespeare did between 1583
and 1592 is not known. Various stories are told. He may have taught
school, or worked in a lawyer's office, served on a rich man’s estate,
or travelled with a company of actors.
One famous story says that about 1584 he and some friends were caught
poaching and were forced to leave town! Or it could be that he was
already in London in 1588, working in the theatre as a page.
By 1592, however, we know that Shakespeare was definitely in London
and vy

already recognised as a talented actor and playwright.
He was then 28 years old.

Be 1592 and 1594, the Plague gaged in London and the theatres

to other types of writing and wrote his earliest sonnets and two long
narrative poems, Venus and Adonis and The Rape of Lucrece. The works
were well received and helped to establish him as a poet. -
