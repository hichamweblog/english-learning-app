“Well, well, this is a surprise. I never thought you were the

“I can’t believe it, either, Lloyd.”

“Just three months ago, we went to the Miner’s Restaurant — ”

The Duke of Shoreditch wanted to sit at the head of the
table. The American Ambassador also wanted to sit at the head
of the table. It was impossible for them to decide, so we had
no dinner.

The English know about the system of precedence.

They have dinner before going out to dinner. But strangers
know nothing about it. They remain hungry all evening.

Instead, we had a dish of sardines 1 and a strawberry. * Now
it was time for everyone to play a game called cribbage. ? The
English never play a game for fun. They play to win or to lose
