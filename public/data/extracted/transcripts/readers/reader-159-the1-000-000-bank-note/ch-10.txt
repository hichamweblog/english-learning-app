As time went on, people began to use coins made of gold, silver,

different sizes and shapes.

Until the 1920s banknotes were backed 2 by gold. This was called
the “gold standard”. 3 It meant that when a person went to the
bank and presented a banknote, the bank gave the person the
same amount in gold. This is why the words “promise to pay”
appeared on banknotes.

Today, coins and banknotes are made in a place called a mint.
Banknotes are made with very special paper. This makes it
extremely difficult for counterfeiters, people who print their own
money, to copy the banknotes. It is against 4 the law, in all
