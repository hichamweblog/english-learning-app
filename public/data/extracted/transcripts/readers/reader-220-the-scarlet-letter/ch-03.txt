In England they were often persecuted and they strongly desired to go to

Company an area north of the Plymouth Colony for settlement.
Initially, the company was considered a business venture, ' but the

In March 1630, Governor John Winthrop led 700 Puritans to the

other ships. The Puritans arrived in Massachusetts Bay and landed
in what is now Salem. Shortly after, Governor Winthrop founded

Boston, which became a prosperous seaport and the capital city.
