Bareback Riding is the original sport of the West. It is a thrilling and
dangerous event where the rider does not have reins to control the
horse. The cowboy must stay on the horse for ten seconds while the
animal jumps and bucks.

2 Steer Wrestling —or bulldogging — is the only event that does not come

from the original Western roundups. A cowboy jumps from his horse to
a steer’s back, takes the steer’s head and pushes it to the ground.

3 Bull Riding is when the cowboy holds on to a long rope that has been

tied around the middle of a bull’s body. A bull ride lasts eight seconds.

4 Calf Roping is still one of the cowboy’s jobs on a cattle ranch when he

wants to catch a calf. The cowboy rides a horse and must throw a rope
around a running calf, stop his horse, get off, throw the calf to the
ground and tie three of its feet. A champion cowboy can do all this in
twelve to fifteen seconds.
