Independence Day

The Fourth of July, or Independence Day, is America’s birthday. This
holiday celebrates the principles of freedom, democracy and equal

opportunity for all.

The American Revolution

The first British colony in the New World was Jamestown, in
Virginia, which was started in 1607. Later, in 1620, a group of people
called the Puritans left England because of religious problems. They
arrived on the northeast coast of North America in 1620 and founded
the Plymouth Colony. In 1630 other Puritans arrived and founded
Salem and Boston. They were hard-working people. They set up
successful businesses like fishing, whaling ! and the fur? trade.

The Puritans believed in education and founded Harvard University
in 1636 in Cambridge, Massachusetts. It is America’s oldest
university.

In the 1700s there were thirteen colonies on the Atlantic coast, all
part of Great Britain. The colonists wanted to be an independent
nation with a democratic government. They did not want to pay
taxes to Great Britain.

In 1773 King George III put a new tax on tea and the colonists were
very angry. A group of men in Boston dressed up like American
Indians and went on to three British tea ships. They threw 342 big

boxes of tea into the water of Boston harbor. This famous rebellion
