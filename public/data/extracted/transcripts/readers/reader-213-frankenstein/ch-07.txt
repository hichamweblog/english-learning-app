Horrified by this news, I said goodbye to Clerval and left for
Geneva immediately. At first | wished to get home as soon
as possible, to comfort my poor family, but when I got close to
Geneva I began to feel afraid. if stopped two days at Lausanne, in a
state of nervous anxiety. Then the lake and the mountains calmed
me, and I continued my journey.

The sight of Mont Blanc brought tears to my eyes. As I
approached my hometown I became afraid again. It was dark when
I arrived, and the city gates were already closed for the night. I
decided to visit the place where William had died and spend the
night in a nearby village. When I got to Plainpalais, there was a
thunder storm. I watched the lightning over Mont Blanc.

Suddenly, in a flash of lightning, I saw a gigantic figure
nearby. It was the monster! Was he the murderer of my little
brother? I began to tremble. In another flash of lightning I saw
