As the days passed, Marianne got worse and worse.
A week later, her sisters persuaded her to go for a
walk. While walking, they saw a gentleman riding
toward them.

“It's Willoughby! 1 know it is!” cried Marianne.
She ran toward the carriage.

It was not Willoughby but Edward Ferrars, the only
person in the world she could forgive for not being
Willoughby. She stopped and smiled, holding back
her tears. As Edward and Elinor exchanged greetings,
Marianne saw their polite yet distant behavior.

When they returned to the cottage, Mrs. Dashwood
greeted Edward warmly.

“So, Edward, what are your mother’s plans for you
these days? Does she still want you to be a politician?”

“No,” replied Edward, “She knows I could never do
that. We'll never agree on a profession for me. I've
always wanted to work for the Church. But that’s too

ordinary for my family.”
