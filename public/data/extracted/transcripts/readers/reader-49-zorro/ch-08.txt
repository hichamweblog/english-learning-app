California Today

California is an important state of the United States. About 34
million people live there. Many old Spanish missions are now
important cities and towns.

schools, and other places often have Spanish names. The capital
of California is Sacramento.

California is famous for its natural beauty. There are tall
mountains, green valleys, forests, sandy beaches and hot deserts
