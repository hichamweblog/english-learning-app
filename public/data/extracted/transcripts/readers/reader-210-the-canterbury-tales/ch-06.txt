Past Simple and Past Perfect

When we are writing about the past, we can use the Past Perfect form
of a verb to show that one action happened before another action. For
example:

FIRST; Palamon escaped from prison.
THEN: He went to the forest.
After Palamon had escaped from prison, he went to the forest.
