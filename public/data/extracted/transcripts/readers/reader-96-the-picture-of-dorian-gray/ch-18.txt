As he left the room, Dorian Gray smiled to himself. Poor Basil!
How little he knew of the true reason. And now he understood
more the painter's wild and jealous feelings, and he felt sorry. There
was something tragic in a friendship so corrupted by passion.

He rang the bell to call his servant. He had to hide the portrait
immediately. It had been mad of him to leave it in a place where it

could be discovered by his friends.
