When Aylmer mentioned the birthmark Georgiana was always
very unhappy.

Aylmer worked many hours in his laboratory with the help of
Aminadab. One day he showed his wife a small crystal ball.

“What is this?” asked Georgiana, looking at the small crystal
ball with a gold-colored liquid. “It is very beautiful. It looks like
the potion of life.”

“In one sense it is. It is the potion of immortality. It can
prolong life or destroy life.”

“Why do you keep such a terrible potion?” asked Georgiana in
horror.

“Oh, don’t worry, dearest Georgiana,” replied Aylmer. “Look
at this powerful cosmetic for the skin.”

“Are you going to use it on my cheek?” asked Georgiana.

“Oh, no. Your cheek needs something that will go deeper,
much deeper.”

When she looked in the mirror and saw the red birthmark, she
hated it more than anything.
