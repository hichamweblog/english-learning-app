The afternoon slowly became night. It was time to go

“All right, I have no choice. But be here early on the

“Yes, sir. Thank you, sir.” Bob left quickly before
Scrooge could change his mind. Once he was outside,
he forgot all about the work — it was Christmas Eve.

He was happy to be going home to his family.
