Russian cities in the 19th century. Peter

“gateway to Europe”. The city is situated

on the coast of the Gulf of Finland, which

provides easy access by ship to the countries
of northern Europe. Peter the Great and his allies built huge
palaces, which still stand today, for themselves. Ornate and
beautiful churches were built here and there among the houses

and government buildings.
