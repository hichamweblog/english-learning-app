waZulu is one of the nine provinces into which South

Africa is divided. The original name KwaZulu means Place

of Heaven in the Zulu language; the name Natal comes
from the Portuguese navigator Vasco da Gama, who discovered
the place on Christmas Day, 1497; da Gama called it ‘Coast of
Natalia’ or Natal.
Natal was occupied by the British in 1694, and later by the Boers
(of Dutch origin) in 1837. A British colony from 1893, it became
a ‘dominion’ of the South African Union in 1910.
Once a magnet for the tall ships, and caravans of imperialists and
fortune-hunters, this great wealth of earthly beauty and resources
is now firmly established as one of the world’s most protected
places. An extensive network of nature reserves and game parks
not only ensure the flora and fauna an abundant future, but also
offer absolute delight to travellers.
