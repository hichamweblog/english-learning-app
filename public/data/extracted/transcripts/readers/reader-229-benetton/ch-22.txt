Are these statements True or False?
(a) Toscani's magazine Colours advertised Benetton and
Sisley clothes.
(b) The largest Benetton superstore in the world was in
Saudi Arabia.
(c) Ahmad Samha helped Benetton open new stores in
Syria and Iraq.
(d) Only the wealthy people in the Middle East could afford
to buy Benetton clothes.
(e) 'Benetton USA corners' were new Benetton shops in
which sold clothes specially designed for the American
market.

Write one of these letters.
• A letter to Benetton saying why you would be a
suitable person to work for the company.
• A letter to Benetton complaining about one of the
advertising campaigns you have read about in the
book.
