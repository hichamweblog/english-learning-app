Present Perfect

‘You have never seen anybody like me before.’

The Present Perfect is formed with the present tense of have or has +
the past participle. It is a combination of the past and the present, and
often provides a strong connection between the two. We can use it to
talk about an action that began in the past and continues up to the
present.
