Past Continuous

One Christmas Eve Scrooge was sitting in his office.

The verb was sitting is in the Past Continuous (Past Simple of to be +
present participle) and describes the continuation of an action in the
past. Look at these examples.

Early that morning the birds were singing in the garden.
Our cat was watching them. (description)

Scrooge was working in his office when his nephew Fred came in.
(the continuing action started before the simple past action and was
interrupted by it.)
