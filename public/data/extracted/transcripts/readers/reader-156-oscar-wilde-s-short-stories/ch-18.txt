Some people thought her husband
was an artist and some people
thought he was a musician. But
his identity was a mystery.

Some men took him while his
mother was sleeping. The men
left the baby with a very poor
family.

He fell in love with all the
beautiful things around him.

The people of the city talked
about him: they said that he
spent all his time admiring
statues. ‘Beauty and art are the
most important things for him.’
He was so fascinated with
beautiful objects that he wanted
more of them. He sent merchants
to India to buy ivory and jade. He
sent men to Persia for silk
carpets and others to find amber
in the north. He sent servants to
look for green turquoise in the
magic tombs of the Egyptian
kings.

The room was full of silver and
gold and beautiful colours.
