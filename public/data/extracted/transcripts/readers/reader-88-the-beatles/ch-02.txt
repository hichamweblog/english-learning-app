The four boys from Liverpool were all born during the Second
World War. Life in the northern city wasn’t always easy after the

like many ports, Liverpool was open to new ideas. Sometimes
ships brought something that other places in Britain couldn't
get - rock and roll records from the US! For the young people
of Liverpool in the 1950s, the exciting sound of rock and roll

was an escape from their daily lives.
