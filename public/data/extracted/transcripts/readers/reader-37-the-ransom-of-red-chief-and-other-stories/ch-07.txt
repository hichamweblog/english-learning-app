Grammar

Much, many or a lot of?
* Muchand many are used to tell us that we have a lot of something

+ Weuse much with uncountable nouns (e.g. much food, time)

I don’t have much time today

* Weuse many with countable nouns (e.g. many trees, dogs and cats)
There are many dogs in the park
- Analternative to much or many is a lot of. A lot of can be used with both

countable and uncountable nouns. It is better to use much and many in
negative sentences and questions:

| don’t have much money

Are there many people here?

However, it is better to use a lot of in positive sentences:
$1,000 is a lot of money

Yes, there are a lot of people here
