Grammar

Comparatives and superlatives

+ To make the comparative for one-syllable adjectives such as old or short
we add -er (older, shorter)

+ For words like big we double the consonant (bigger)

+ With adjectives ending in y we change the y to -ier (happy — happier)
For adjectives with two syllables or more we put more in front
(interesting — more interesting)

* To make the superlative of one syllable adjectives we use the word the
and add+est to the end of the adjective (strong — the strongest)
