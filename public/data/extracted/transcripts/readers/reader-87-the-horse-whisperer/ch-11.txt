Grace watched them untie the ropes. Pilgrim lay there for a

Tom told Grace to lead him straight to the water at the side of
the arena. She stood next to him while he took a long drink.
Then Tom joined them. He prepared the horse for riding and
told Grace to get on. She put one foot up. Pilgrim didn't move.
Tom took her weight and she lifted her leg over the horse's back.
She felt no fear. She walked him first one way around the
arena and then the other. It was some time before she heard the
others. They were laughing and shouting happily; it felt just like
the day she rode Gonzo.
But this was Pilgrim. Her Pilgrim. He was better. And she
