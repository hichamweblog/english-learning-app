Picture palaces
The greatest of all the ‘picture 
palaces’ huilt in the 1920s and 1930s 
was the Roxy Cinema in New York. 
It cost $12 million to build and had 
6214 seats. Three hundred people 
worked at the cinema, with sixteen
projectionists and 110 musicians. It 
also had a hundred singers, and a 
group of dancers to keep the 
audience happy - and its own 
hospital! The Roxy opened in March 
1927 and closed in March 1960.
