Glossary
age a number of years, or a period 
of time in the past
audience a group of people 
watching or listening to something
bend make something into a 
different shape
civil war a war fought between 
different groups of people in the 
same country (the American Civil 
War, 1861-1865)
contract an agreement in writing 
between people
fashionable something which is 
popular at the time (often clothes)
flicker shine on and off quickly
glorious wonderful
heaven where God is believed to 
live, and where good people are 
believed to go when they die
invent make something new - for 
the first time
magic something strange and 
wonderful
microphone something which is 
used to record voices or sounds
project make a film or picture 
appear on a screen using a machine 
(projector)
queue a line of people waiting for 
something
scenery things on a stage which help 
to make it look like a real place
screen the part of a cinema or of a 
television where the pictures appear
script the written-down story of a 
film or play
shot a single picture seen in a film
studio a room where films are made
system a way of working or doing 
something
war fighting between different 
countries (or between groups of 
people within a country - civil war)
