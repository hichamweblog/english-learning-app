A New Face at TrentTV

David Fox’s son, Simon, had just graduated from
university with a degree in Media Studies and to

David’s joy, had decided to follow in his father’s
footsteps. David was therefore a happy man when

he met his senior employees on Monday afternoon.
He had just explained to them about Bruce Kemp’s
franchise offer and what TrentTV had to do to win it.
“That’s fantastic news,” said Rav, the technical
director. “Just imagine, Australians will soon be able
to watch the programmes that we produce.”

Jenny Walker, the director, was more cautious. “We
haven’t won yet. Don’t underestimate Anna Webb.

I know her well and she’ll do everything she can to
get the franchise.”

David looked at the three people in the room.

He had met Jenny two years ago when she worked for
ChannelWebb and had immediately offered her a job
at TrentTV. Jenny had accepted and Anna Webb
hadn’t forgiven either of them. As director, Jenny
made the decisions and gave the orders to Rav. It was
his responsibility to push the right buttons at the right
time. Sally Truman was the production assistant and
was extremely efficient. She made sure that everyone
involved in the programme was ready and in the right
place.

“We are a good team and I believe we can win,”
