Near evening they come to a wide valley. There are no plants here,
only rocks and gravel. There isn’t any mist now. The last of the
sunlight shines on the snow of the mountain summits. In front of
them there is an enormous wall of green ice — the glacier!

Nutu walks towards it. He studies it for a long time, then he starts
to climb up the ice wall. Evidently, he’s searching for something.
Then, he waves urgently to Neil and Julie, turns towards the ice
again and suddenly — disappears!
