FH ut the Ghost wants to go on again. He says to Scrooge, “Hold my
robe,” and they pass over the miners’ field and out to the sea.

The sound of the Waves is very loud and a fierce wind is blowing from
the north. Scrooge thinks, “Where is this Spirit taking me?”

Soon they see a lighthouse in the distance. Seaweed clings to the
bottom of it and the cold waves beat against its walls. There are two
‘men inside the lighthouse, sitting near a fire. The light from the fire
is bright in the darkness. The men shake. hands and wish each other
Merry Christmas. They drink their beer together. One of the men
‘begins to sing a Christmas carol. His face is red and tough from the
‘sea air. Then the two men tell each other stories. They are laughing.
They feel the Christmas spirit even in this remote place.

he Ghost takes Scrooge to a ship sailing on the sea. The
Captain and his crew are eating together. The captain asks

all to sing a Christmas song. Some of the men are talking about
Christmases past. Some of them are talking about going home to see
‘their families. Every man on the ship is thinking about Christmas,
and feeling the Christmas spirit move in them. They want to be

kind to their companions and share their happiness.
Scrooge is perplexed. Why are all these people so happy?

“Spirit, why are you showing me these scenes?”
asks Scrooge.

“What do you see in them?” the Ghost asks.

“I see happy people everywhere,”

answers Scrooge.

“My time is almost finished,” says

the Ghost then. “Touch my robe.

It is time to go.”
