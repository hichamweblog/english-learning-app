When The Wizard of Oz was made

‘into a movie, some details were
changed. One of the most famous
changes was changing the color of Dorothy’s
magical shoes. In the original story Frank Baum wrote,
her slippers were silver. But the director decided that the
slippers should be red in the movie. This was done to
take full advantage of the new color technology sweep-
ing the film industry in the late 1930's. As a result, most
people who saw the movie, even if they had also read the
story, would say that Dorothy's shoes were red, not

silver!
