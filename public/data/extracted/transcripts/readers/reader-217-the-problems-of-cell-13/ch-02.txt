hisholm prison was a large building. It was four

space. The wall around it was six metres high and
impossible to climb. Even if a man managed to
escape from his cell, he could not pass over the wall.

The yard ' around the building was eight metres wide on all
sides. This was the distance from the prison building to the wall.
During the day, prisoners used the yard to do exercises. But it was
not for those prisoners in Cell 13.

There were always four armed guards in the yard, one for each
side of the building.
