Chi'en are sticks that show your future. People use them to ask
the gods for advice when they don't know what to do. The
chi'en have long and short lines cut into each side. You throw
the sticks on the ground and read the pattern they make.
Different patterns have different meanings. One of the patterns
means "yes."
Hsiao-pao laughed to himself when he heard what Mr. Yeh
was praying about.
