strong, with a long black beard, a dark face and evil eyes.

The Reverend Mr Glennie, who was the parson and our
schoolteacher, knew a lot about these things. He said that
Blackbeard’s real name was Colonel John Mohune. During the
Civil War, King Charles | was a prisoner and gave him a huge
diamond in exchange for a favour: to help him escape. Mohune
took the diamond, but did not help the king escape. He hid the
diamond somewhere and after the war he returned to
Moonfleet, where everyone hated him.

Mohune died and the secret of the diamond died with him. So
now his ghost was trying to find the precious diamond.

However, Mr Glennie did not believe that Blackbeard had

The past perfect is used to show that something was finished or
completed before another action began. It is often used in stories to
give the background. In the example above, John uses the past perfect
to show that his parents died long before the beginning of his story.

The past perfect is formed in this way: had + past participle.
