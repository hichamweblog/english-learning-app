CHAPTER ONE

The Mohunes

Moonfleet is a village about half a mile from the sea on the west
side of the Fleet stream. The village is separated from the
English Channel by a lagoon and a big beach.

When | was a child | thought the village was called Moonfleet
because on a calm night the moon shines brightly on the lagoon.
But later | learned that the name comes from ‘Mohune-fleet’;
the Mohune family were once important people in the area.

My name is John Trenchard and | was fifteen when this story
began. My parents had been dead for years and | lived with my
aunt, Miss Arnold, a kind but strict woman.

It was late in October of 1757 and I was sitting in the living
room reading a book called The Arabian Nights’ Entertainment.

street. It was a poor street because Moonfleet was a poor village,
