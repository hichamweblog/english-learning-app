“We're not here to answer questions,” said Elzevir angrily.’

The word angrily in the example above is an adverb of manner. It
describes the way Elzevir spoke to Aldobrand. In other words, he spoke
to him in an angry way. Adverbs of manner are usually formed by
adding -ly to adjectives. In the case of words like angry, which have
more than one syllable and end in a -y, the y is changed to an i.
