fter all these intrigues, Mrs Jennings declared that she was

and see her comfortably settled there. And, she reminded
her sister, Cleveland was only eighty miles from Barton and
their own dear mother and little sister. After staying with
Mrs Jennings for a short visit, they could then return home
by a quicker route to Barton Cottage.
