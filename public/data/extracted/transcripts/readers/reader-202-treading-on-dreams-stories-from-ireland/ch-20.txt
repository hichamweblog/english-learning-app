Irish music is well known throughout the world.
From Sydney to Buenos Aires, from London to
‘New York, you can hear an Irish song, dance to
reel, and take a drop of Irish whiskey.

Itis a sad thing, though, to see an Irishman
far from home who is too fond of his glass...

'y name is Michael Coleman and they say I am the finest

fiddler that ever lived. They say I put a twist to a tune —I

‘of where the twist comes from, I play that way because it is the

My home is a small room in the South Bronx in New York,

‘fiddle and played a couple of tunes, and then there was no
