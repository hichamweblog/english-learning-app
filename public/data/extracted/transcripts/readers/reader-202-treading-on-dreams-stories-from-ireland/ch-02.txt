‘When he was a stone’s throw from the door, I saw that he
vwas coloured. In those days, packmen were quite common in
country areas, They went from house to house with their cases
of clothes and bedsheets and cheap jewellery, and if a customer
had no money to buy, the packmen were usually willing to take
food instead. They had a name for being dishonest.

"The sight of this packman put the fear of God into me,
because Mother had taught us to keep away from all packmen,
and I had never seen a coloured man before in my life. I led
Granny to the jow and hid behind her.

“Will he attack us?’ I whispered fearfully.
