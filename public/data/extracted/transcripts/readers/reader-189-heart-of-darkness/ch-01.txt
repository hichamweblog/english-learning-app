t was evening, and the Nellie, a yawl,! was anchored in
the Thames. We looked down the river that flowed to the sea.
London was behind us, a great black shape.

The captain of the Nellie was a company director. The other
guests included a lawyer, an accountant, myself — and of course

Marlow. We all knew each other well, and we all shared a passion
for the sea.

We looked out on the river and remembered its history.
Famous men and famous ships had sailed out from here to
perform famous deeds. 2 They had sailed out to fight in battles,
to conquer other countries, and make fortunes. The Thames had
carried men to the places of their dreams, and it had carried men
who were inspired by greed.

‘This has been a dark place? as well,’ Marlow said suddenly.
