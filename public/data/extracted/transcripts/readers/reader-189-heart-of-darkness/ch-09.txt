‘He made me see things’

Look at these sentences with ‘let’ and ‘make’:

— He made me see things = He caused me to see things.

— He made me give him the ivory = He forced me to give him the ivory.

— He let me see the ivory = He allowed me to see the ivory (or, He gave me
permission to see the ivory).

Notice that both ‘let’ and ‘make’ are followed by the direct object and
an infinitive without ‘to’.
