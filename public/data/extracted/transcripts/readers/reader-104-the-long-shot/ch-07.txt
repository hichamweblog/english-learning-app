ootball, baseball, and basketball are three of the most

important sports in the United States. Basketball is played

in the winter months, usually from October until April.
Every school and college in the United States has at least one
basketball team and the games are important social and sporting
events. Basketball games are usually held on Fridays or Saturdays in
the evening. The whole school comes to watch the game and cheers
for its team. Each school team wears its gwn special colors and has
a particular name. For example, the Chicago Bulldogs or the Salem
Tigers.

Before every game starts and during the game, cheerleaders lead
the crowd in singing songs and cheering for their team. The girls and
boys who are the cheerleaders wear special clothes in their team colors.
They clap their hands and stamp their feet and do special dances in
time to the songs. The cheers are usually short rhymes.
