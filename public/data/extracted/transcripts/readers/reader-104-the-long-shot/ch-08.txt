ames Naismith was born in 1861 in Almonte, Ontario,

Canada. In 1891 he is working as a physical education

teacher at the YMCA school in Springfield (Massachusetts).
He thinks about inventing a new sport for the students. It must be an
indoor sport because the winters are too cold to play outside. He thinks
about using some of the sporting ideas he learned in Canada. He tries
organizing soccer and lacrosse games inside but it is too difficult;
the players need more space. Then, Naismith thinks of something
different. He attaches two peach baskets to the balconies at both
ends of the gymnasium. He invents thirteen rules for the game.
There are two teams with nine players on each team. Each team
must run and pass a soccer ball between them and try to throw it into
the basket hanging on the wall. The new game is called “basketball”.
In 1895 Naismith moves to Denver to work at another YMCA
school and starts basketball teams there. Later, he directs the physical
education department at the University of Kansas (UK), where he
lives for 36 years. He dies in Lawrence, Kansas, in 1939.
