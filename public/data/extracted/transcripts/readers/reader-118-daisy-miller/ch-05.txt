It was a beautiful sunny day. At the castle, they walked
around alone except for the guide. Winterbourne asked the guide
not to hurry, to let them pause wherever they pleased. He gave
the guide a generous tip 3 as he said this. The guide thought he
was being paid to leave them in peace, and he did so.
