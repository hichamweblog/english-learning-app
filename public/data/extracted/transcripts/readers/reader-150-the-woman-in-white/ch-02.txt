Late the next evening, | arrived at Limmeridge House. Mr

down to breakfast at nine. The dining-room was long with

beautiful figure. She turned and walked gracefully towards me.
‘What will her face be like?’ | asked myself as she got nearer.

After breakfast, | went to see Mr Frederick Fairlie in his room.

‘Laura,’ said Miss Halcombe, ‘you have your drawing book

‘No, I’m afraid to begin,’ said Laura gently. ‘I know my
