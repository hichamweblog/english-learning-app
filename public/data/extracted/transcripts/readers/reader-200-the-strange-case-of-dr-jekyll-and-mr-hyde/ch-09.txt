visited this area and knew its people well. The divided nature
of Scotland’s capital with its highly moral professional classes,
doctors, lawyers and clergy in the New Town, and the
desperately poor in its older quarters, is reflected in the double
identity of the main character in Stevenson’s book. Dr Jekyll is
a respected, morally unquestionable doctor but his gentleman’s
