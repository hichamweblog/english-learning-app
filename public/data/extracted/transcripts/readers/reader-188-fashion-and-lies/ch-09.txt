What happens at Gucci shows that success in the fashion business
depends on a number of factors. A fashion house needs to hire the
best designers on the market and support talented young designers.
You also need to create an image: "People are buying into your
dream," Ford explains. "A pair of black trousers is a pair of black
trousers. If you go to the Gucci world you're buying into a different
world from the Versace world. To create that world takes the soul
and personality out of a designer." Finally, you need to learn to handle
stress, rumours, rivalry, jealousy and have a strong business sense.
