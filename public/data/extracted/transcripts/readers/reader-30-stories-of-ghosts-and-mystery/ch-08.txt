Rudyard Kipling (1865-1936) was born in Bombay, India. '. o
When he was six he was sent to school in England. At the age of
seventeen Kipling returned to India and became a journalist for

Kipling was a great writer; he wrote for people of all ages and of all

places. His most famous works include: The Light that Failed (1890),
