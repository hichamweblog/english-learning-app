It is very common to talk about things that already happened. If something already happened or is
finished it is in the past. Below you will find some sentence starters and example sentences to help
you talk about things you have done in the past.
Most Common Verbs Used to Talk about the Past
I went…
I ate…
I was…
I read…
I played…
I saw…
I talked to…
I worked…
Example Sentences:
I went to work.
I ate spaghetti for lunch.
I read two books over the weekend.
I saw a movie.
I talked to my friend.
