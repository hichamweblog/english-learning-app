Time is important. In the United States it is customary to be punctual or arrive on time to places. It is
not common to arrive late or after a designated meeting time. It is considered rude to arrive late.
When reading a clock, it is customary to state the exact time. Some people round up but telling the
exact time is more common.
For example:
2:00= It's two o'clock.
10:15= It's ten fifteen.
4:30= It's four thirty. (In British English uses 'half past' instead of '30'.
6:45= It's six forty-five. or it's a quarter to seven.
11:25= It's eleven twenty-five.
If it's almost a time you can say….
It's almost _____.
1:57 = It's almost two.
