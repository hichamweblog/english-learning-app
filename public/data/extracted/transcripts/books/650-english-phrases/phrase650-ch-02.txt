How you say good-bye in English depends on who you are talking to. There are formal, informal and
VERY informal ways to say good-bye.
Similar to greetings, some people shake hands, wave, hug and kiss but this varies from situation to
situation. It is best to follow what other people are doing or to do what makes you comfortable.

Good-Bye

Good night!

Bye
