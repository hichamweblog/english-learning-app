7.4 Joining Sentences
Conjunctions can also combine two sentences, just as they join
words or phrases.
• and expresses addition
The following example shows and used as a conjunction between
two sentences.

We went to the pool.
The Miller family went to the parade.

We went to the pool, and the Miller family went to the parade.

8.1 What Is an Interjection?
Interjections are sudden, interrupting words or phrases that
are also known as exclamations. Common interjections include
wow, well, hey, bravo, and oh.
An interjection expresses strong emotion or surprise;
it functions independently within a sentence.

Wow!
Look at that!

In the example above, we see that the interjection Wow! stands
apart from the rest of the sentence and adds an element of
surprise.
Interjections are often signaled
by an exclamation mark.

Example: Bravo! You won the race!
