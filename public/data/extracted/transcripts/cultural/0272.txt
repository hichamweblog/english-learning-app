Well, the letter "g" actually has a lot of different meanings in English; it's used
informally for a couple of different things. The most common modern use, within
the last 25-30 or so, is for it to stand for another word that begins with a "g",
which is "gangster," or as it is pronounced in the slang "gangsta." A person who
is a "gangster" is in a group or a gang of criminals, people who do things that are
illegal. However, it has become, through movies and songs, more popular so
that sometimes you'll hear just men particularly, or boys, say, "Hey G, what's
going on?" It doesn't necessarily mean that person is a gangster. Again, it
depends on the group that you are with. I would never use the term, but people
who are younger, for example, might use it informally. But originally, it was an
abbreviation for the word "gangster."
An older meaning of the letter "g" is that it is an abbreviation for "a grand," or
1,000 dollars. You could say, "I spent 20 Gs on my car," meaning I spent 20,000
