Cassius, "Cassius." Cassius from Brazil wants to know the meaning of the verb
to figure out. To figure, "figure," out, "out, " two words. To figure out is one of
those phrasal verbs, or verbs with two words in it that is very popular in English.
To figure out means to solve a problem, to understand something that you didn't
understand before, to find a solution to a problem. These would all be ways of
defining the verb to figure out. For example, I was talking to my wife yesterday. I
figured out that she was very angry with me. And the reason I figured it out - how

didn't say anything back to me. I figured out she was mad, she was angry at me.
You can also figure out a math problem, or figure out the answer to a puzzle,
something that you have to try to find a solution for. So, that is the meaning of
the verb, to figure out, and we use it very often.
